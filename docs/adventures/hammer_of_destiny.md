# Hammer of Destiny

<div className="tiny">(572 PC) </div>

Hired by General Blackheart, four adventurers journey into a deep forest to retrieve a weapon of great power. They rend it from the hands of a sorceress, defeated her great golem, and delivered the hammer to the General, giving him his signature weapon, allowing him to win the War of Eclipsing Fire
