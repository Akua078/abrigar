<div className="tiny">(573 PC) </div>

<div>
    ## Session One - Rock Bottom

    The Party meets in the 'Rock Ring', thrown into a tournament for varying reasons. Iapyx rewarded them as winners, offering them a high reward job escorting a ship called The Compass Rose on its journey to Portgaard. Intrigue strikes as Cyris Go'el recognizes the weapon Catastrophe carries, and Empira discovers Cyris' ancient knowledge 
</div>
</div>

<div className="c_left">
<div>
    ## Session Two - Skeleton Crew

    The Party escorts The Compass Rose towards Portgaard, but are attacked in the middle of the ocean. Iapyx himself was found as a stowaway, blasting a hole open in the bottom of the ship and sinking it, several water elementals flooding in to retrieve the cargo and attack the party. They barely made it out, but managed to save a few inert antimatter rifles, and some documentation from the captain's office. Aatrox is warned to never return to Stormwreck Isle, his mother referring to it as 'hers'. Empira is haunted by an unknown entity, and a strange sigil of an eye has appeared above her forehead. Catastrophe is faced with a huge security breach, having uncovered a cache of weapons being manufactured without Maddock approval.
</div>
<div>![Session Title Card](/img/adventures/los2.png)</div>
</div>

<div className="c_right">
<div>![Session Title Card](/img/adventures/los3.png)</div>
<div>
    ## Session Three - Homebound

    The Party travels to Fermelios, Empira's hometown, and hunt the libraries there for answers. The party meets the people of Fermelios and profit from their adventures, stocking up for the journey ahead. Empira learns of others who share the affliction, the strange sigil on her forehead, and decides to go to them for answers. 
</div>
</div>

<div className="c_left">
<div>
    ## Session Four - Old Friends

    The Party track down three ex-true souls, Moon Meadows, Dorian Blackheart, and Iapyx Maluna, and learn more about True Souls, about their own pasts, and reveal hidden truths that threaten to destabilize everything they thought they knew. Catastrophe learns the identity of the traitor, Cyris the fate of his people, and Empira the source of the greatest secrets in Abrigar. Aatrox Menethil prays to his mother for guidance, and is told to hunt Cooper Cortez.
</div>
<div>![Session Title Card](/img/adventures/los4.png)</div>

<div>![Session Title Card](/img/adventures/los5.png)</div>
<div>
    ## Session Five - Every End

    The party stumbles into Cooper Cortez's ruined castle, searching for answers. They discover what has become of Cooper Cortez after his years, and fight for their lives. Knight Maddock is slain by the beast that Cortez has become, and the party is thrust into the past, 573 years displaced, to find the truth about their secrets. The goddess of time herself is left to fight Maddock herself. 
</div>
</div>

<div className="c_left">
<div>
    ## Session Six - Seven Days

    The Party meets with Zephyr, several ancient true souls, and bear witness to the end of the before times. They attempted to save Varuhm, or any of its people, and saved only one. They ran, fleeing to the Temple of Drifting Mist, and wept for the carnage.
</div>

<div>
    ## Session Seven - Zero

    The party attempt a dangerous mission through the forgotten peaks, making a beeline for The Maddock Clan as it begins. The party learns much of the ancient past thanks to the books they found in Bloodlace Palace. Cooper Cortez is revealed to have stolen the name of an ancient vessel of a god, and The crimes of every Maddock are listed in a strange book. The party is left to decide to either follow Cortez, or Maddock. 
</div>
</div>

<div className="c_left">
<div>
    ## Session Eight - Ancient Sands

    The party makes the difficult decision to chase Maddock to the wastes in hopes of an ally. They come to ancient Saacili, and rent dune foxes for the journey through the sands. They are ambushed by a predator as ancient as the world, and struggle to survive. 
</div>

<div>
    ## Session Nine - Fire at Will

    Cyris Go'el dies at the hands of a Zaratan. The party is almost wasted in the desert before being saved by a new friend, who leads them to Anh. In Anh, the party groups up with Maddock, and claim their ally. The Party and Maddock work together to make a plan to attack Cortez where he lives. The party spend years warring with Cortez, and readying their army. 
</div>
</div>

<div className="c_left">
<div>
    ## Session Eight - Loving Memory

    The Party defeats Cooper Cortez, reducing him to a monster that can barely hold a human form. He donned a mask, one matching the Court of Laughter Masks worn by the group. Ulric secured his circle and rebuilt. Catastrophe slew the Shadow Weaver and now knows how to keep him down, avenging Knight Maddock. Empira became a world renowned scholar and historian, spreading the word of her findings across the land. Aatrox was accepted back into his home and to his mother's service. A statue is erected of Cyris Go'el at the monastery he protected, long since lost. 
</div>
