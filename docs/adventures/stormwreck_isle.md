## Summary

Stormwreck Isle is a small island off the coast of Fratera. It is said that a great red dragon died below this island centuries ago, and so it draws dragons and dragonborn from all around the world. Not much is actually found on the island, though, other than these fighting dragons.

## Session 1

In 572 PC 3 people requested passage to the island. Dorian Blackheart, the son of Thrain Blackheart, the current general of Arutu, Ulthrak, infamous member of the Cult of Gunnmar, and Dua Leapa, a bard seeking his father. Dorian sought to make a name for himself, as his older brother Sebastian had always been his father's favorite.  Ulthrak had been sent to the island by his patron, Gunnmar the Black, to secure a magic item that was being used for a ritual there. Dua sought his father on Stormwreck isle because this island was his last known location. 

The Party requested passage from a small passenger vessel manned by two sailors, Bucket and Leg. With minor financial compensation the group traveled to Stormwreck Isle, but were ambushed upon arrival by a horde of undead. The Captain, Leg, was lost and Bucket was left incapacitated with shock. The party was left stuck on the island for the moment, though they had made landfall at Dragon's Rest, a small village containing the only legitimate population on the island. The village elder, Elder Runara, greeted the party with open arms, and allowed them to stay in the village provided they made no trouble.  

The party spoke with the townspeople, meeting the population of kobolds, along with a few humans. Notably, a war veteran named Varnoth, a retired criminal turned alchemist Tarak, and a small, ill kobold child named Frub. Frub claimed to see a dragon fly to the island, Tarak mentioned that his supply of alchemical supplies was blocked by a large monster, and Varnoth mentioned seeing a ship wreck on the  north side of the island.

## Session 2

The party left Bucket at Dragon's Rest, and went off on their way. They encountered Sebastian Blackheart, also on the island, here to outshine his brother in any way he could. How he got to the island was at first a mystery.  Sebastian planned to attack the party, but Dorian's determination was enough to scare him off until he had a proper plan or advantage. 

The party  left to help Tarak first, seeking access to his supply of potions, and proceeded to Seagrow Caves on the southern end of the island. There they found a Spore Servant Octopus blocking access to a cave, as Tarak described. They slew the octopus, and ventured into the cave. There, they found a population of Myconids, who had been trapped in the cave by their Octopus. They informed the party that they had been trapped since the artefact that kept their guard, the octopus, tame. They said they saw a group of Kobolds take it, but they couldn't see where they went. With that the party returned to Dragon's Rest. Though they saw Sebastian head this way, they did not find him in the village. They spent the night in the safety of the cloister.

The next day the party decided to track down the dragon that Frub claimed to have seen, having seen it themselves from the ship on their way in.  On their way they were attacked by a starving owlbear who had a small wooden whistle around its neck. Ulthrak seized the whistle and used it to tame the owlbear, taking it as a companion and naming it Owlbert. 

They proceeded to the center of the island, where they found a Moonstone Dragon watching over a fey marketplace called the Seelie Market. They explored the market, meeting the dragon that looked over it, and the various shop keeps. They acquired a deck of wonders, several avian companions, and had their fortunes told by the goblin fortune teller. Also, they found that Sebastian Blackheart had recently travelled with the Seelie Market, requesting passage from Avernus, and it appeared he had traded a very sentimental sword hilt to do so. 

## Session 3

The party left the Seelie Market to return to Dragon's Rest, and arrived without issue. They sought out Sebastian, hearing from Frub that he had taken refuge in a nearby abandoned tower, but did not find him. The Party proceeded to investigate the shipwreck Varnoth had seen, taking most of the day to travel there. Owlbert, however, refused to go near the water, revealing his hydrophobia. 

Within the shipwreck the Party found several undead and dispatched them. They searched the ship for any reason it may have wrecked, but found nothing. When they were preparing to leave, though, they were attacked by several harpies, who's song had drawn the sailors to the shore. They proceeded to kill the harpies, but before they could be defeated the harpies grabbed Dua and dragged him into the sky. Dua, in mortal danger, saw a feline figure offer to save him. Remembering his fortune warned him to trust those who offered to help Dua allowed the cat to save him, returning him to the ground safely. The figure saw the party, and appeared to be interested in their doings, though it vanished. 

The Party found several items in the wreck, but most notably, they found a picture of Dua's father in an observatory on the eastern reaches of the island and a small idol that appeared to be the origin of the undead scourge on the island. Upon breaking the idol the zombies plaguing the island vanished, leaving the party in relative safety.

The Party gathered themselves and left the shipwreck, making camp in the forest for the night. They were attacked though, by Sebastian. Dorian and Sebastian fought, and Sebastian lost quickly, though in the fray Sebastian revealed that he had magical powers that he gained from his journey into Avernus. Dorian could only watch as Sebastian was dragged away to Avernus, having evidently violated some term of his contract by losing the fight. 

The Party determined to return to Dragon's Rest in the morning, then proceed to the observatory to find Dua's Father. 


## Session 4

The Party returned to Dragon's Rest. They determined to ask Tarak about his connections to the thieves guild he was a part of, and to speak with Varnoth about her military past. After these interactions the party rested again, though they didn't sleep for long. The cloister was ambushed by Sebastian, possessed by his patron, and a few kobold warriors. The enemies destroyed most of Dragon's Rest before waking anyone, setting the village ablaze. The Party fought off the assailants, but they escaped on the back of a mysterious dragon who took them east, towards the observatory. Varnoth was slain in the battle, and Elder Runara revealed her true form as an Adult Bronze Dragon. The Party gave chase to the enemies, leaving Dragon's Rest in the care of Elder Runara. 

The Party arrived at the observatory and made their way inside, witnessing a ritual taking place. The dragon that had saved Sebastian and the kobolds from the Party appeared to be the subject of the ritual, and they remained to protect him in his ritual. The Party and these agents of the dragon, Sparkrender, fought a glorious battle, and the Party was ultimately victorious. Dorian managed to snap Sebastian out of his domination, and Ulthrak took the magic item from Sparkrender, preventing the ritual as his patron wished. Dua searched the aftermath of the battle for his father, but could not find him. Instead, the party found that this observatory had been a research facility that was looking into some form of planar travel. Dua's father left his personal log behind, detailing his day to day activities, and confirming that there was some sort of accident that transported him to an unknown plane of existence.

Dua resolved to keep searching for his father, even if it took him to another plane. Dorian decided to stay on this journey with Dua to bring himself even more glory, and Ulthrak stuck around for company and adventure. The party found several magic items around, including figures of wondrous power and an amulet of identify caster, that could come in handy trying to find Dua's father. 

Preparing to leave, the party gathered again on the battlefield, but were approached by a man in a purple suit. With no indication of how he arrived, the man informed the party that they had 'defied fate', and insisted they showed him how they did it.  The man, after being prompted, introduced himself as Barry, the god of fate. The party could not tell Barry how they had defied fate, not knowing they had done so themselves, and Barry warned them that he would be watching. The small white cat that had saved Dua in the shipwreck also appeared, revealing its name as Ankh, Barry's underling. 

Barry and Ankh disappeared, leaving the party to return to Dragon's Rest again, and leave the island with Sebastian as their new sailor.
