## Arc 1 - Rocky Beginnings
<div className="tiny"> Sessions 1 - 3 </div>

Roric Night-Vane finds himself washed ashore after the wreck of the Tide's Folly. Saved by Admo Cisra, an old friend and crewmate, the two set off to hunt down and enact vengeance upon whoever dared to attack their crew. They join forces with Alasha Kaloa of the legendary ship building family, and recruit two other crew mates. Doreen Green, a lost orphan girl seeking her place in the world, and Beff Jasos, a debtor on the run after a few less than ideal decisions. Together, they set off in search of coin to fund their ends, and of their destinies on the waves. 

Admo tells the crew of a bounty he has taken on from the Fang Collectors, and the crew agrees this is a good place to start looking for gold, and to get to know one another. The journey will take them along the southern channel, below the mainland of Abrigar and through dangerous waters. They stop at Moddina, the capital of the Fang Hunters, to collect supplies and meet their patron, one Cael Ulia working under the Azure Corporation. Cael promises the crew 5,000 gp upon collection of a 'Coral Core' from a monster that one of their experiments let loose. He says he will meet them on the island it has been located on to collect it, but that the hunt is all theirs. The island is called Maqar, located north of Anaxin, and it is known for being barren, and difficult to find. To this end, Cael gives Admo a compass to point the way to the island, and wishes the party luck on their voyage.
