Abrigar is a world full of danger and mystery. Thousands of individuals and groups have dedicated their lives to traveling and exploring every inch of Abrigar, to fighting its dangers and searching its depths. These individuals are known as adventurers. They can be hired very often to solve problems, to slay beasts, or investigate anomalies by anyone from peasants to generals to kings or kids. This is a catalogue of adventures travelled by many heroes, some finding their way into history books, some slipping into obscurity. Either way, these are their stories.
