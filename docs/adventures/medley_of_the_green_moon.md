    # The Medley of the Green Moon

    <div className="tiny">(572 PC) </div>

    A party of adventurers are tricked into a twisted game of fetch by a malevolent fae named Quaai, who claims to be working for 'The Chef'. They compete against two other teams of adventurers in a race to the death, until only one team makes a meal worthy of a great spirit of misery. The party races through Abrigar, gathering various food items while fighting many monsters of the Green Moon as well as their fellow contestants, gathering ingredients to make a twisted stew for The Chef, ending with a final confrontation where they create the medley itself, as The Chef, revealing his name to be Chef Mi'eke, helpfully guides them.
