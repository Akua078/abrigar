# The Vanguard: 

# The Amethyst Blight

<div className="tiny">(573 PC) </div>

The world is facing an escalation of threats that traditional armies, city guards, and local guilds simply cannot handle. From corrupted elemental beasts tearing through the frontier, to rogue archmages attempting reality-altering rituals, to planar rifts threatening the natural balance—the world is fragile, and the usual authorities are entirely out of their depth. 

We are the Vanguard. We are a self-governed, elite alliance of powerhouses. We don't take orders from kings or mayors; we step in when a threat is deemed worthy of our intervention. We protect life, preserve the arcane balance, and prevent the end of the world. We answer the call.
