<div className="intro">
The Whispering Woods are one of the few places in Abrigar that remain largely untouched by the wider world. Dense forests brimming with magic stretch across the northwestern corner of the mainland, sheltering hidden glades, ancient secrets, and dangers unknown. The Woods are home to numerous Druidic circles and other folk who prefer to live apart from the bustle of civilization. Though the circles are fiercely independent and frequently at odds with one another, they share a common interest in preserving their home. Together, they maintain a fragile peace that allows the Woods to stand apart from the nations surrounding them, though what exactly makes them a nation remains difficult to define.
</div>

  ## Emblem

  The Emblem of the Whispering Woods is unofficial. It was created by a hired Gactan artist, mostly for international records. For this reason, it is fairly simple and impersonal, green and brown representing the trees and wood. The artist had little understanding of the druids in the woods, only the literal geographical climate, and because of this the emblem is almost universally hated. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  The Whispering Woods lie against the northwestern corner of the mainland, forming a densely forested region largely untouched by the wider world. The thick canopy conceals countless secret glades and dark corners, hiding treasures and enemies alike. Travel through the Woods is notoriously difficult, with the dense growth making passage challenging for most outsiders. Druids, however, often guide trusted individuals through the forest. Some claim the Woods are cursed to turn away unwelcome travelers, twisting their paths until they lose their way. Such tales are generally dismissed as legend.
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  The Whispering Woods were not founded as a nation, but grew into one out of necessity. For generations, the Druids lived within the forest, tending to its magic and keeping to their own affairs. As the surrounding nations began to form and expand, borders had to be established between their lands and those of the Druids. Faced with the threat of being swallowed by their neighbors, the Druidic circles were forced to set aside their differences and organize themselves politically. Thus, the Whispering Woods became a nation, united more by necessity than by any shared desire for governance. The circles remain fiercely independent, bickering among themselves even as they recognize that their collective survival depends on maintaining their fragile unity.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  The Woods operates as one entity politically, but is actually several dozen druid circles spread out in the trees. Each circle sends a representative to meet in a grand council of druids twice a year, and this council decides when and how to do things such as wage war, levy tax, and protect the woods. This meeting is almost always full of angry bickering, and most often not all of the representatives return unharmed. 
  </div>

<div>
## International Relations

The Whispering Woods are recognized as a nation by several of the surrounding states, despite lacking a conventional government or unified leadership. Their collective strength lies in the Druidic circles' willingness to defend their shared territory against outside interference. Relations with the wider world remain complicated. The Woods are largely left to their own affairs, and their isolation has allowed them to preserve their independence. However, their lack of a clear political structure can make diplomacy difficult, as no single authority can reliably speak for every circle. Their relation with Morgana and Farith remain stable, at least, for trade. Though many merchants ride through the woods faster than necessary, just in case. For all their internal disagreements, the circles understand that their survival depends on maintaining a united front against external threats. Any nation seeking to claim the Woods must contend not with a single ruler, but with a collection of powerful Druids who may disagree on nearly everything else.
</div>

  ## Capital

  The Whispering Woods has no capital city, really. The largest city is Mosshaven, easily, but the nature of the many circles in the Woods prevents a capital being officially claimed. Even so, when a major meeting needs to happen between the major circles, it usually ends up happening in Mosshaven.
</div>

<div>
  ## Mosshaven

  Mosshaven was a great druidic power in the woods, harboring the Circle of Wildfire primarily. The Druids of Mosshaven ensured that peace was kept in the forest using their army of wildfire spirits. They used their connection to the forest to communicate between circles and play arbitrator to many conflicts. 

  Mosshaven was attacked by monsters of the Shadowfell, summoned by Marlamin Arulo in a test of faith. Barry wanted Mosshaven destroyed so that it could not keep the peace in the forest, such that the forest would turn to a land of chaos and strife. Barry wanted to remind any true souls that happened to return of what they did, and to hopefully scare them off. Marlamin accomplished this by sabotaging an experiment by planar wizards trying to create a portal to the far realm. Marlamin used his connection to the weave to alter the source of the spell, causing the souls of the wizards to be used instead of the magical items they had prepared, and send what was left of their bodies to the Shadowfell. Marlamin took the source of the ritual, a blue, blade shaped gem that creates portals, and cut the fabric of reality to create the portals to the Shadowfell. A second attack occurred with another chosen of Barry, but its effects were reversed through an act of divine intervention, brought on by Moon Meadows. The victims of the second siege were returned to life, though with white eyes and scars, and they elected Moon Meadows as their arch druid in thanks. Now, the city heals and grows even more powerful than before. 

  Archdruid Moon Meadows is focussing on reconstruction efforts in Mosshaven. By her side is Tao Raqua, a non-binary tabaxi who fled from their Lewen homeland in fear of persecution. Tao deals with the people, serving as the face of the reconstruction, though Archdruid Moon is still extremely well liked.

  ## Saduh Tani

  Saduh Tani is a small fishing town at the northern end of The Wastes. This town acts as the only connection between the sea and The Null, and as such is a critical economic point for the area. In the Red Age, Visia pummeled this town specifically, targeting the ports and merchants, to try and economically cripple the rebellion. Because of this, the town is still highly damaged, and the adjacent waters filled with shipwrecks that could give Broken Mast Bay a run for its money.
