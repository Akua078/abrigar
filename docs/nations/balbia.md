<div className="intro">
Balbia rules the northern sea, as well as the Balbian Island Chain. They are the fishermen of Abrigar, as well as a primary source of more variety based foods, such as rice, fruits, and vegetables rather than grain. Their main island, simply called Balbia, is the main source of this food, despite its icy, snowy nature. Fire magic is prevalent here, greenhouses littering the white landscape, growing green like emeralds on a plane of snow. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  The Balbian Emblem is used as a flag as well, but mostly to emblazon shields for their army. The war with Farith was hard fought, and their blue and white shields camouflaged them in the snow, a cunning strategy from the white bound hunters. Now, it remains as a symbol of patriotism. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Balbia has taken the northern sea of Abrigar, as well as the Balbian island chain inside it. A portion of the mainland is taken too, a spoil of war with Farith some years ago.
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Balbia emerged similarly to The Woods and Farith , claiming what land major nations had not, though it had its scuffle with Farith over the island chain and the shores of the mainland, which it ended up winning ultimately. Farith is still disgruntled about it, but the Farithian government is too disorganized to properly retaliate.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Balbia is one of only two democracies in Abrigar, the other being Visia. They elect a Chancellor to lead them, whose term lasts five years before another election is held. A Chancellor that has previously run is not allowed to run again except in special circumstances, and it is generally considered dishonorable to run twice. 
  </div>

## International Relations

Balbia has fairly good international relations, though tensions with Farith remain from their war centuries ago. Balbia engages with much trade, especially with Koral, though their rivalry to rule the oceans keeps them from being too friendly. 

Balbia holds good standing with nations that they trade with often, being Minera, Visia, Fratera, and Kulcin. 
</div>

  ### The Mysst Spit

  The smallest Arutuan lake. It lies in between the other two, used mostly as a water route deeper into Arutu, and even into Venom Lake to access The Whispering Woods and Eplem. Fishing is quite the attraction here, the lake being filled to the brim with silver fang carp, a kind of carnivorous fish known for its silver scales. 
</div>
<div>
  ### Inkittu

  Lake Ironscale is the first lake a ship comes to when traveling up the Gnok River System, going west to Arutu. Its known for its strategic position, serving as a hard border between Arutu and Haldaerok. What little navy Haldaerok has is stored here, ready for deployment to Fracture Lake, usually. Riverswatch is settled into the Northwest corner of the Lake, serving as a control point for the river leading to deeper Arutu and beyond.
