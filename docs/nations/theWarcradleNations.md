<div className="intro">
The Warcradle Nations are the three nations just east of Arutu, north of Lewe. They have been locked in a war since before recorded history began. The reason for the conflict has been long forgotten. Still, the Warcradle Nations duke it out, refusing to lose no matter the cost. It is thought that the warcradle was once united as one nation, as evidenced by many cultural similarities, most notably their emblems. This idea is not generally accepted in the warcradle itself, however, as most find the idea disgusting.

The Warcradle Nations have been at odds for ages, but the specific reasoning was lost just as long ago.  The War is a stalemate lasting centuries, but each nation still has its unique fighting style. Ammar, being the only one with water access, has a strong navy, using the rivers and lakes of the area to their advantage. Ammar seeks to control the water, having taken Lake Red from Dravenmor in the last movement of the borders centuries ago, and now is fighting to stop Dravenmor from accessing the Seven's Inlet and to control the Gnok river themselves. Haldaerok is a nation of brute force. They have the most land, and the best relationship with Lewe at large, meaning they have a near endless supply of soldiers. They throw waves of disposable troops at Dravenmor and Ammar, aiming to take over the land by sheer force of will, and they don't shy away from using dirty tactics. Dravenmor is much worse, though. They're willing to do anything at all to win, often using child soldiers, targeting innocents, and enslaving armies. They don't shy away from their monstrous actions, and their sheer cunning and battle strategy give them the edge they need to defend from both opponents.

  ## Government 

  Each Warcradle nation is ruled by a King or a Queen, who also serves as a general and commander of the armies of that nation. In this way, similar to Arutu, the terms 'General' and 'King' or 'Queen' are used interchangeably. Generals rule without challenge to their power, but political assassinations are so common that all a rival really needs to do is wait. A General rules with total authority, and most with an iron fist, ensuring brutality and ruthlessness for their people.

## The Capitals

<div className="c3">
<div>
## Dubki
![Debki](/img/nations/dubki.png)
<div className="tiny"> Ammar </div>

Dubki is the capital of Ammar, though many argue it should not be. It used to sit nearer to the border, but since the push north of the Ammaran border, many argue it should move north as well, closer to Lake Red. Its a relatively underwhelming capital, dwarfed in power even by other cities in Ammar, such as the many along the coast that hold all the trade of the nation. Dubki remains the capital, despite its flaws, as Ammar clings to tradition and its people hold fast to their patriotism. 
</div>
<div>
## Unngh
![Unngh](/img/nations/unngh.png)
<div className="tiny"> Dravenmor </div>

Unngh is the capital of Dravenmor, and the place where they scheme, plot, and keep their armies of prisoners and slaves. The few live here, those in charge of the nation and the less than a thousand who are actually willfully present in the nation. This is the capital of war crimes, but the sheer design and strategic construction of the city makes it absolutely impenetrable.

The building you see in the image above, lovingly illustrated by Sir GPT, is simply known as the Cobalt Keep. No one who's ever been inside has ever said anything of their time inside, though most don't come out at all. This is the prison, the fortress, and the center of Dravenmor. 
</div>
<div>
## Presham
![Presham](/img/nations/presham.png)
<div className="tiny"> Haldaerok </div>

Presham is a city after Venice's own heart. The streets here are mostly water, taking full advantage of the Gnok river splitting at its doorstep. Presham is a hub of trade and shipping for the war-hungry nation, though mostly soldiers arrive. The place is riddled with recruiters from the army, drawing the desperate and the angry to fight for the growing army of Haldaerok.
</div>
</div>

<div className="c2">

<div>
  
  ## Carbi

  Carbi, the city in the center, is little more than ruins. Almost the entire city is dust, leveled by constant war. It was once a great city, theorized to be the capital city of a once united warcradle, which would explain why all three of these nations insist on fighting over it, though no one remembers why. Now, its ravaged constantly by fighting and war, never safe, and covered in corpses and blood.

## The Warcradle Lakes

<div className="c2">
<div>
![Fracture Lake](/img/nations/fractureLake.png)

### Fracture Lake

Fracture Lake serves as a border between Dravenmor and Haldaerok. It's hotly debated territory, and serves as the main point of conflict between the two nations. The lake bed here is riddled with shipwrecks, to the point of small islands emerging from the water, made of splinters and wreckage. Thousands of soldiers give their lives for this lake, but its ownership has eluded both of its borders. 
</div>

<div>
![Lake Red](/img/nations/lakeRed.png)

### Lake Red

Lake Red used to be the border between Ammar and Dravenmor, before Ammar made a bit of progress and took the other shore with their superior navy. Now, it serves as a trophy for Ammar and its citizens, though the water is still tainted red from blood, and the sand on its beaches is mixed with bone.
</div>
