<div className="intro">
The Commonwealth of Morgana, or just Morgana, is a collection of five city states that have banded together for protection and power. Nestled in the northwest corner of Abrigar, they've made good friends with the druids of The Whispering Woods.  They are largely self-sufficient, detached from the aggression of Arutu and the economic dominance of Minera and Fratera,  and they stand together as a beacon of hope for those who would see peace in the world.  Each city state protects each other, from threats internal and external, each state ensures its sovereignty. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  The Emblem of Morgana is a shield, purple and black representing the magic and metal that keeps the nation safe. The antler in the middle has five points, representing the five cities that came together to form the Commonwealth. The deer's antler is symbolic of the nature of Morgana, the wilds in its borders as well as its alliance with the neighboring druids. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Morgana is settled in the north western corner of Abrigar,  behind the Whispering Woods. It's coast is huge, but most of it just faces The Ice. It has rivers running deep through it, resulting in a strong emphasis on boats for travel.  Its position allows it protection from most other nations, as does its relations with the druids to the east.
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  The Commonwealth of Morgana formed long ago, in the first hundred years of the written history of Abrigar.  Marcus Bell, an infamous warlord known for his brutality, ruled over the land for the first century. The details are not known, but a hero known only as Morgana made a mysterious sacrifice, which led to Bell's demise. In the spot of Bell's death, the town of Bell's End was founded, and the Commonwealth formed. It was created and remains an alliance between five city states, spreading their combined territories over the north west of Abrigar, vowing to protect one another from threats within and without. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Morgana's government is a collection of five lords, sometimes called kings or sovereigns, that each rule over one of the city states that form the nation. These five lords get together to make important decisions in the name of the commonwealth, but otherwise each state is left to govern itself.
  </div>
  <div>
    ![The Morganan Lords](/img/nations/morgananLords.png)
  </div>

<div>
## International Relations

Morgana seldom organizes with other nations, other than the druids of The Woods. When they do, they typically send a representative or five, depending on the severity of whatever issue is for discussion. The Commonwealth has a positive relationship with The Woods, their two nations walking through history hand in hand, so much so that many other nations consider the unorganized woods effectively under the jurisdiction of The Commonwealth.
</div>

  ## Capital - Bell's End

  Bell's End serves as the primary artisanal hub of the commonwealth, renowned for its master craftsmen, bustling marketplaces, and centuries-old traditions of fine workmanship. From blacksmiths and weavers to alchemists and scribes, the city thrives as a center of innovation and trade, drawing merchants and artisans from across the land. Beyond its cultural significance, Bell’s End also functions as the logistical center of the state, ensuring the swift mobilization of supplies, weaponry, and personnel should an army ever be required. With its well-fortified granaries, extensive road networks, and strategically positioned garrisons, the city stands as a testament to both artistic excellence and military preparedness, making it an indispensable pillar of the commonwealth. 
</div>

  ## Portgaard

  Portgaard is a thriving coastal city built upon the relentless toil of its fishermen and the boundless bounty of the sea. As the largest fishing city in the northwest, its harbors are constantly teeming with vessels, from humble rowboats to grand seafaring ships, all bringing in a daily haul that sustains the region. The city is famed not only for its abundant seafood—feeding towns and settlements far beyond its borders—but also for the rare and valuable oceanic treasures that occasionally wash ashore or are dredged from the deep. Whether it be shimmering pearls, ancient shipwreck relics, or mysterious deep-sea curiosities, Portgaard’s riches extend beyond mere sustenance, making it a place of both commerce and legend. Its docks bustle with traders, its markets overflow with the freshest catch, and its people live by the rhythm of the tides, ever connected to the unpredictable vastness of the ocean. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Fermelios

  Perched on a jagged cliff side, Fermelios is a mountaintop town dedicated to knowledge, study, and the preservation of ancient magic. The town’s architecture is a layered sprawl of marble terraces, copper-domed monasteries, and spiraling towers. Mist rolls through its narrow alleys, smelling faintly of old parchment and candle smoke. Every other building is a library, scriptorium, or temple to forgotten knowledge gods, and the streets are crawling with robed scholars, errant monks, and cursed interns carrying stacks of grimoires bigger than themselves. Tucked into redwood clad forests and mountainous crevasses, Fermelios is a sanctuary of knowledge. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Coshpolia

  Coshpolia is a rugged, industrious town built upon the relentless labor of its loggers and miners. Nestled amid dense forests and rocky hills, it serves as one of the primary suppliers of raw materials to the region, ensuring a steady flow of timber and stone for construction, infrastructure, and trade. While its mines lack the glittering allure of Minera’s famed treasure troves, they are essential nonetheless, producing high-quality stone and quarried rock vital for building cities, fortifications, and roads. The vast forests surrounding Coshpolia are not places of untouched beauty but of industry, where the rhythmic echo of axes and saws fills the air as lumberjacks fell mighty trees destined for shipbuilding, carpentry, and fuel. Life in Coshpolia is one of grit and endurance, where the people carve a living from the earth and wood, ensuring that the foundations—both literal and figurative—of Abrigar remain strong and steadfast. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Valencia

  Valencia is a tranquil, agrarian town nestled in the temperate southern plains, where rolling fields of golden grain stretch as far as the eye can see, and the lowing of cattle echoes across the countryside. Rich with hardworking farmers and skilled ranchers, Valencia serves as a vital supplier of grain, meat, and other essential goods to the surrounding regions, ensuring the prosperity of both rural settlements and bustling cities alike. Beyond its agricultural importance, the town stands as a key crossroads, linking the southern reaches to the rest of Abrigar. Travelers, merchants, and messengers frequently pass through its well-worn roads, stopping at its modest inns and lively markets before continuing their journeys. Though Valencia is quieter than the grand cities of the realm, its steady industry and unwavering reliability make it an indispensable part of Abrigar’s lifeblood, sustaining the land with both nourishment and connection. 
  </div>
