<div className="intro">
Visia is a large nation in the northeastern corner of Abrigar. Its one of the few democracies in Abrigar, choosing to be a rather peaceful state in the interests of its people. This nation exports crops mostly, but provide magical products and services. Druids who aren't loyal to a circle or to the Woods tend to flock here, seeking the lush deltas and fertile soil. Visia tends to stay out of violent conflict, but an unspoken tension exists between them and the Wastes, which they insist is not a nation on its own, instead claiming it is simply an illegitimate rebellion in their own borders. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Flag

  Visia's flag represents the lush, green wetland that makes up most of the nation, with blue rivers crossing to represent fertility and crop growth. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Visia is nestled into the northeastern corner of Abrigar, containing most of the Visian Deltas , and completely surrounding the Null Wastes. Many forests cover the landscapes, claiming Visia as a largely untamed corner of Abrigar despite the advancement of its people. The deltas run through these forests, creating a lush environment for those seeking more peace than most of Abrigar can provide. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Visia emerged as a nation of refugees, mostly fleeing the Warcradle Nations and Arutu as they formed. The people here are peaceful, and consist of wood elves, loxodon, and firbolgs for the most part, as well as a lot of less common races, those minorities taking shelter here from the wider world.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Visia is one of the last democracies in Abrigar, ruled by a president, often a native wood elf. Their policies rarely change, each president priding themselves on stability and a lack of hostility, ignoring the malice of the Wastes.
  </div>
  <div>
    ![CAPTION](/img/nations/visianPresident.png)
  </div>
</div>

<div>
## International Relations

Visia is a fairly peaceful nation, producing food and using its vast shoreline to trade with Balbia and Koral, and to distribute food to the rest of the world. They surround the Null Wastes, which, if considered a nation, would hate them. Though, no nation recognizes the Wastes as anything other than an anarchous nation like Lewe, or at best, wilderness, so their international reputation isn't much affected. 
</div>

  ## Capital - Chipton

  Chipton is a lush city, full of trees, rivers, and fruits. Its tucked into the main visian deltas, providing fertile soil, used here for agricultural magic and so called 'civilized' druidic magic. The seat of the government lies here, though they don't do much but protect those who run to them. 
</div>

<div>
  
  ## Costlacot

  Costlacot is the economic center of Visia. Ironically far from the bulk of the country, and of Abrigar, this market serves as a sort of refuge from the high stakes or intense capitalism of cities like Dorado or Kirch. Costlacot is surrounded by farms in the rich, fertile deltas of the Visian countryside, producing the main income for the nation.
