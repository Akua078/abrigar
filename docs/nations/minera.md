<div className="intro">
Minera is a shining beacon of golden paradise sitting in the center of the mainland of Abrigar. It prides itself on economic dominance, and its capital, the 'Golden' Dorado, is easily the busiest port in the world. Its rivals are few, and Minera uses its sway to control politics all over the world.

The people of Minera are wide and varied, with goods and races from across Abrigar mixing and mingling, sharing ideas and religions.  The majority of people here are humanoid, but that's about all one could safely assume about an individual in Minera. The nation has good relations with its sister nation, Fratera, just to its east. Together, they hold massive power over the world. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  Minera's emblem was created by their highest class, and reflects the wealth of those peoples. The yellow background and orange circle reflects the rising sun, depicting Minera to dawn of all things. The orange circle is also meant to represent a coin, reflecting Minera's mercantile status. The black triangle represents the forgotten peaks, overshadowed intentionally by the golden coin, underplaying the suffering of the Mineran miners. As well, the black shield pattern reflects how the peaks are the backbone of the nation, a silent acknowledgement to keep the enlightened of those oppressed peoples sated.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Minera is located in the center of Abrigar, just south of the Forgotten Peaks. Some of Minera brushes up against and into The Peaks, consisting of mining towns extracting the valuable metals in the mountains. It is north of Lewe, and nestled against the warcradle nations, though it maintains full control of its heavily armored convoys through the more troubled regions. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Minera was formed in the beginning of Abrigar, the nation itself ancient. Not much is known of its early history, but legends say it was extremely hostile for hundreds of years, before abruptly turning to trade with anyone who would be willing to buy. It is unknown how much of this history is true, but the nation now stands as a force to be reckoned with, both militarily and economically. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Minera is a republic, a ruling council of their most powerful, laying the laws over their lands.

  Typically, a spot on the council is given to a merchant that becomes obscenely wealthy, or a previous member 'disappears' and has their spot silently filled by a new merchant who has 'no idea' what happened to them. The populace does not trust the government, as it usually only protects merchants and promotes monopoly. 
  </div>

<div>
## International Relations

Minera is usually indifferent to most nations, offering loyalty in exchange for money. The loyalty of their armies can be purchased,  and they don't shy away from that. In the war of eclipsing fire Minera switched sides frequently as Arutu and Eplem each offered more and more coin.

Fratera is known as a sister nation to Minera, the two engaging in much trade. Minera supplies raw materials and rare metals from the peaks, and in exchange, Fratera supplies magical items and goods. These two nations are the economic heart of Abrigar, Minera's raw materials and Fratera's arcane technology propelling the world forward. Together, no nation can challenge this pair, not alone, anyway.
</div>

  ## Capital - 'Golden' Dorado

  Dorado, nicknamed 'Golden' Dorado, is the capital city of  Minera. It is known for its tight security and valuable commodities, and is easily the busiest city in Abrigar. Dorado claims a huge amount of naval trade, rivaled only by Portgaard in service of the mainland. 
</div>

<div>
  ## Varuhm

  Varuhm is a mining town in the northernmost part of Minera Just south of the town is Musta Grotto, where the inhabitants of Varuhm mine for silver and platinum. The town is mostly inhabited by poor, impoverished lowlifes with no better chance than to mine for ore, and hope they find something valuable. Some miners have come out of the grotto with gems or found large deposits of ore that they refuse to share. Those lucky few go on to become rich, and leave Varuhm forever. 

  Rumors of this chance at fortune draw unfortunates from near and far to try their hand, meaning Varuhm is full of both homeless and Inns, both of which are dirty and in need of repair. The Iron Sword is aware of Musta Grotto, and new recruits routinely go there to try their hand in the dangers of the mine, or to investigate the rumors of planar travel. Because of these rumors, Varuhm is easily the biggest mining town, despite being in horrible condition relative to other options. Those who are desperate would even risk walking planes to find a way out of the hole they have made in their lives.

  ## Fragua

  Fragua is like a second capital to Minera, but very different from Dorado. Where Dorado is a capital city of commerce, Fragua is a city of manufacturing. A blacksmith’s heaven, Fragua is filled with forges and factories, built in old quarries so deep one struggles to see the bottom. The area Fragua is in was once volcanic, and so is filled with basalt, which the townspeople mined. Because of the black rock making up the region, Fragua has been called “Dark” Fragua. Ironically, because of all the forges, Fragua is actually quite bright, even at night.

  Fragua was once a mining town, like Varuhm, but even poorer. The citizens, more often refugees with nowhere to go, mined basalt in massive quantities. Eventually though, Michael Maluna came to Fragua and showed the people a better way. The Fraguans used magic to process raw ores coming in from all over Mineral and made them into bars and coins, polished gems, and cut bricks. From then on, the quarries equipment fell to ruin, and the quarries themselves filled with the sounds of hammers on metal. 
</div>

<div>
  ## Dahaba

  Dahaba is the biggest mining operation in Abrigar. A mix between the poverty of the mining operations in the peaks and the riches of Minera at large, this town is the dream of the powerful and the desperate alike. Precious metals and gems come from here, crates of it daily. The only thing more profitable than mining here is being hired to defend from the ludicrous bandit presence, as just one crate of riches would be enough to allow a lucky thief to live in luxury.
