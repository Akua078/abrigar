<div className="intro">
Minera is a nation built on cultural and economic divides, sitting in the center of the mainland of Abrigar. It prides itself on economic dominance and its capital, the 'Golden' Dorado, is easily the busiest port in the world. Its rivals are few, and Minera uses its sway to control politics all over the world. Their wealth, though, comes from the backs of their people. While the merchants and nobles of Minera sit high on their thrones, their gold is generated by the broken backs of the poor under them. The Mineran system is not merely backwards; it is corruption incarnate. 

The people of Minera are wide and varied, with goods and races from across Abrigar mixing and mingling, sharing ideas and religions. The majority of people here are humanoid, but that's about all one could safely assume about an individual in Minera. The nation has good relations with its sister nation, Fratera, just to its east. Together, they hold massive power over the world. 
</div>

  ## Emblem

  Minera's emblem was created by their highest class, and reflects the wealth of those peoples. The yellow background and orange circle reflect the rising sun, depicting Minera to be the dawn of all things. The orange circle is also meant to represent a coin, reflecting Minera's mercantile nature. The black triangle represents the forgotten peaks, overshadowed intentionally by the golden coin, underplaying the suffering of the Mineran miners. The black shield pattern acknowledges the Forgotten Peaks as the backbone of Minera's prosperity. It serves as a quiet recognition of the miners' importance, offering symbolic respect to the very people whose suffering sustains the nation's wealth.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Minera is located in the center of Abrigar, just south of the Forgotten Peaks. Some of Minera brushes up against and into The Peaks, consisting of mining towns extracting the valuable metals in the mountains. It is north of Lewe, and nestled against the warcradle nations, though it maintains full control of its heavily armored convoys through the more troubled regions. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Minera was formed in the beginning of Abrigar, the nation itself ancient. Not much is known of its early history, but legends say it was extremely hostile for hundreds of years, before abruptly turning to trade with anyone who would be willing to buy. It is unknown how much of this history is true, but the nation now stands as a force to be reckoned with, both militarily and economically. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Minera is a republic, a ruling council of their most powerful, laying the laws over their lands. This council is called the Council of Coin, and they rule with an iron fist over the weary and starving populace.

  Typically, a spot on the council is given to a merchant that becomes obscenely wealthy, or a previous member 'disappears' and has their spot silently filled by a new merchant who has 'no idea' what happened to them. The people distrust their government, which primarily protects wealthy merchants and encourages monopolies. Yet widespread poverty makes organized rebellion difficult, leaving the Council of Coin secure in its position. The council understands this well. Despite their rivalries, its members ensure that the working class remains dependent on them, preserving the very conditions from which they profit.

## International Relations

Minera is usually indifferent to most nations, offering loyalty in exchange for money. The loyalty of their armies can be purchased, and they don't shy away from that. In the war of eclipsing fire Minera switched sides frequently as Arutu and Eplem each offered more and more coin. Similarly, in the Koralian Feuds, Minera supplied soldiers to only which side could pay, switching sides so rapidly their own men were often confused, and a few of their ships even ended up firing on one another. 

Fratera is known as a sister nation to Minera, the two engaging in constant trade. Minera supplies raw materials and rare metals from the peaks, connections to the outside world, and infrastructure. In exchange, Fratera supplies magical items and goods, crafting the supplies provided by Minera into magical items both commonplace and legendary. These two nations are the economic heart of Abrigar, Minera's raw materials and Fratera's arcane technology propelling the world forward. Together, no nation can challenge this pair, not alone, anyway.

  ## Capital - 'Golden' Dorado

  Dorado, nicknamed 'Golden' Dorado, is the capital city of  Minera. It is known for its tight security and valuable commodities, and is easily the busiest city in Abrigar. Dorado claims a huge amount of naval trade, rivaled only by Portgaard in service of the mainland. The city is built with shining gold and marble, with beautiful architecture and towering spires overlooking the shining sea and bustling ports. The walls are built so high it is impossible to see the top when standing at their feet, and the gates are guarded by only the strongest warriors. To merchants, Dorado isa safe haven, a bustling city of opportunity. To thieves, Dorado is the ultimate gamble, because in Dorado, the punishment for theft of any kind, from grand larceny to pickpocketing, is death. 
</div>

<div>
  ## Varuhm

  Varuhm is a mining town in the northernmost part of Minera Just south of the town is Musta Grotto, where the inhabitants of Varuhm mine for silver and platinum. The town is mostly inhabited by poor, impoverished lowlifes with no better chance than to mine for ore, and hope they find something valuable. Some miners have come out of the grotto with gems or found large deposits of ore that they refuse to share. Those lucky few go on to become rich, and leave Varuhm forever. 

  Rumors of this chance at fortune draw unfortunates from near and far to try their hand, meaning Varuhm is full of both homeless and Inns, both of which are dirty and in need of repair. The Iron Sword is aware of Musta Grotto, and new recruits routinely go there to try their hand in the dangers of the mine, or to investigate the rumors of planar travel. Because of these rumors, Varuhm is easily the biggest mining town, despite being in horrible condition relative to other options. Those who are desperate would even risk walking planes to find a way out of the hole they have made in their lives.

  ## Fragua

  Fragua is like a second capital to Minera, but very different from Dorado. Where Dorado is a capital city of commerce, Fragua is a city of manufacturing. A blacksmith’s paradise, Fragua is filled with forges and factories, built in old quarries so deep one struggles to see the bottom. The area Fragua is in was once volcanic, and so is filled with basalt, which the townspeople mined. Because of the black rock making up the region, Fragua has been called “Dark” Fragua. Ironically, because of all the forges, Fragua is actually quite bright, even at night. The city provides much of the metal to Fratera, processing raw ore from the mountains into useable forms for the artificers they work with. 

  Fragua was once a mining town, like Varuhm, but even poorer. The citizens, more often refugees with nowhere to go, mined basalt in massive quantities. Eventually though, Michael Maluna came to Fragua and showed the people a better way. The Fraguans used magic to process raw ores coming in from all over Minera and made them into bars and coins, polished gems, and cut bricks. From then on, the quarries equipment fell into disrepair, and the quarries themselves filled with the sounds of hammers on metal. 
</div>

<div>
  ## Dahaba

  Dahaba is the biggest mining operation in Abrigar. A mix between the poverty of the mining operations in the peaks and the riches of Minera at large, this town is the dream of the powerful and the desperate alike. Precious metals and gems come from here, crates of it daily. The only thing more profitable than mining here is being hired to defend from the ludicrous bandit presence, as just one crate of riches would be enough to allow a lucky thief to live in luxury for their entire life.
