<div className="intro">
Fratera is a magical nation full of sorcery and magics both modern and ancient. Many a great wizard has made their home in the Frateran wilderness or in one of its great cities to which spellcasters flock. Fratera has a symbiotic relationship with Minera,  and generally provides the world with a vast majority of magical items through trade, making the nation fairly prosperous.
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  Fratera's Emblem was created as a symbol of power. The black arrow on the bottom represents the Fraguan range, and the black border represents the security those citizens of Fratera enjoy.  The purple circle in the middle represents the weave, the source of all magic, that powers Fratera. Its form is bent into a circle to represent the magic cycle, how magic is conserved and used by the inhabitants of Abrigar.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Fratera is located in the southeast corner of Abrigar, brushing against the Fraguan Range. It claims a few islands in the sea, but their shores aren't defended extensively. Its massive shoreline allows for much trade of their magical artifacts, but most of their goods travel through Dorado.  
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Fratera was formed in the beginning of Abrigar, the nation itself ancient. Not much is known of its early history, but the nation now stands as a force to be reckoned with, both militarily and economically. Its alliance with Minera is historic, allowing an iron grip on economic trade in the region rivaled only by Koral's complete grasp on the sea. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Fratera is a Monarchy, a grand wizard sitting on a throne in their capital city of Stellae. This position is up for grabs to any powerful enough to challenge the grand wizard, and turning down a challenge is seen as cowardly. Typically, no one challenges the grand wizard unless they know themselves to be stronger, and to have the support of the nation. In this way, their duel is more of a formality, and is often non-lethal.
  </div>

<div>
## International Relations

Fratera is  a nation of trade and manufacturing, many great artificers emerging from its cities. Fratera is known for producing magical goods, ordinary and extraordinary, for every corner of the nation. It is said that magic flows through the air in Fratera, and its arcana attracts wizards and sorcerers from all realms. 

Fratera is known as a sister nation to Minera, the two engaging in much trade. Minera supplies raw materials and rare metals from the peaks, and in exchange, Fratera supplies magical items and goods. These two nations are the economic heart of Abrigar, Minera's raw materials and Fratera's arcane technology propelling the world forward. Together, no nation can challenge this pair, not alone, anyway.
</div>

  ## Capital - Stellae

  Stellae is nicknamed the city of wizards. The place is brimming with magic, and at its heart stands a massive tower, where the grand wizard lives and studies. The city has colleges that many mages young and old come to study in, councils that fund magical expeditions aboveboard or otherwise, and astonishing displays and lectures of magic. 
</div>

<div>
  ## Stormwreck Isle

  Stormwreck Isle is a small island off the southern coast of Fratera. The Island is known for conflict between the chromatic and metallic dragons, though it isn’t known by many outside of this conflict. Many dragons and dragonborn flock to the island because of its history, and because of legend. It is said that a great red dragon was sealed below the island long ago, and so chromatic dragons are drawn to the area. In response, metallic dragons followed suit to disrupt that which the chromatic dragons loved, as their creators would have approved of. Metallic and Chromatic dragons have long been at odds, so this behavior is to be expected.

  There are reports of Harpies and Myconids on the island, though neither have been confirmed to the public. There is a small observatory on the north side of the island, long abandoned. The project lost funding years ago, and its inhabitants were left to an unknown fate, though it is suspected and can be expected that they were killed without the financial protection of their unknown benefactor.

  Fratera lays claim to the island, but few nations would place importance on its defense. There is little material value on the island, though a small settlement called Dragon's Rest is present on its northern shore. No more than a dozen humanoids are confirmed to live on the island, including the inhabitants of Dragon's Rest.
