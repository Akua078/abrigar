<div className="intro">
Koral rules the waves of the Shattered Sea, as well as every body of water in Abrigar that connects to the ocean. Rivaled only by Ammar, barely, Koral absolutely dominates the water with their naval prowess and extreme affinity for creating ships that travel at breakneck speeds.

Koral prides itself on neutrality and trade based diplomacy. They have never actually waged war since the Koralian Feuds that formed them, but economic ruin tends to come to their enemies faster than any army could ever lay siege to a castle. Koral is not a large nation, its armies small and unimpressive, but its ships filling nearly every harbor in Abrigar. To find a port town without Koralian ships is to know that the port you have stumbled into is soon to turn to dust. 
</div>

  ## Emblem

  The Koralian Emblem was created in the midst of the Koralian feuds, to unify the Koralian Archipelago. For this reason, it is very general, blues representing the water, and a ship's wheel representing their navy. Despite the simple and impersonal design, it was effective. Under this banner the Koralian Navy swarmed Anaxin and won the feuds. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## International Relations

  Koral has never waged war on any nation since the Koralian Feuds, though their preferences for trade have been made clear. They exercise their authority not with swords but with gold, refusing trade in massive, organized boycotts that can cripple nations dependent on oceanic trade. Koral has a close relationship with Eplem after their support in the Feuds, as well as an ironically great relationship with the Null Wastes and Balbia through trade and mutual control of naval travel. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  It is said that Koral emerged from a dragon's hoard, that a mighty hero sailed to Abora and rent the loot asunder and became the first to claim the trident for himself. From there, the coffers of Koral grew, and the nation became wealthy and prosperous, the King's ship coated in the dragon's scales to help it skim through the water like a bird sailing through the sky. This ship has never been found, and no evidence of a dragon's hoard graces the royal coffers, but many hold fast to this legend nonetheless. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Koral is a monarchy, the throne lying not in a castle, but in spirit. The King of the land is dictated by the owner of a legendary magic trident, known as the Koralian Trident. The wielder must earn the Trident through honorable combat, but ownership can be challenged at any time.

## Geography

  Koral is an archipelligo nation in the northern ocean of the Shattered Sea. There are many islands in the main Koralian Archipelligo, each rich in history. Abora is the largest Koralian island, containing the capital of Koral and the largest hub of naval trade in the world in Watchleach. Abiro is the second largest island, mostly fishing and private property to house the rich and powerful of Koral. The King lives here, despite the capital being in Watchleach, for the peace and quiet. Aryo and Ayo are, arguably, the two least significant islands in the Mawqa region. Small fishing islands, both of them, they refuse to grow and expand with the rest of Koral due to a long standing debate. Their names sound too similar, and each both demand the other rename their island. These names and this conflict have stood for hundreds of years, and is highly controversial in Koral. Most of the nation calls these people fools, as well as the world. Rumor has it, two brothers founded the islands. Each brother is said to have taken his island and named it for their family name. Luckily, one of the brothers was so stupid he didn't remember his own surname correctly. One day, historians hope to find out which, and determine which name is correct, and which island must change. Acel and Adek are the meat of Koral, the largest fishing economies outside of Ishkari, exporting an enormous amount of fish. Some even speculate that they cheat by fishing in the elemental plane of water, but no evidence of that has been found. It is rare that they get visitors, but they supply a huge amount of the world with seafood. Abic and Abusiel are the primary manufacturers of naval vessels in Abrigar. Ports surround the island, and artificers wishing to hone the craft of oceanic travel come from far and wide hoping to strike it rich. The Akwiyacwiny Atoll (ah-kwee-yah-CHWEEN-ee) is said to be the most peaceful place in Abrigar. Coral reefs are protected from harsh ocean currents inside the atoll, creating a lush environment for rare and exotic fish species. Culinary delights come from this island, recipes protected fiercely, dishes enjoyed daily. Angwen serves as a travel hub in Koral. Ports surround the island, and it is known for being a stop just before entering the main depths of the Koralian archipelago. These islands, together with many smaller ones, make Koral what it is, an economic powerhouse still riding the high of winning the Koralian Feuds.

  ## Capital - Watchleach

  Watchleach is a city that rivals even Stellae in its construction. The whole city floats on a small spit of land off the shore of Abora, the roads built to last eons on the calm waves there. The place is a marvel of engineering, all powered by a blue gem in the center of the town hall, a gem surging with such magic that the entire city has been without a stormy day in centuries. Docks mark every inch of the shoreline, boats coming and going so frequently that the waters are unsafe to even fish in, for fear of catching another vessel.
