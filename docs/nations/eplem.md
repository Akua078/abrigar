<div className="intro">
To the west of Abrigar lies the realm of Eplem, a land once renowned for its rich culture, powerful magic, and vibrant cities. However, Eplem's reputation has been tarnished by the recent conflict with Arutu, which resulted in the loss of a significant portion of its territory. Despite this setback, Eplem remains a land of wonder and mystery. Its cities, though scarred by war, still boast a unique blend of medieval and ancient architecture, reflecting the nation's long and storied history. The streets are filled with the sounds of merchants haggling, scholars debating, and musicians playing enchanting melodies. And in its capital city, islands made of stone and riddled with farmland float high over the streets. 

Eplem's true strength, however, lies in its mastery of magic. The nation is home to some of the most powerful spellcasters in the known world, their knowledge passed down through generations of arcane scholars. Eplem's mages are renowned for their mastery of both offensive and constructive magic, and their spells are feared by many. However, the conflict with Arutu has taken a toll on Eplem's magical prowess. Many of its most powerful mages have fallen in battle, and the nation's magical infrastructure has been damaged. Eplem is now struggling to rebuild its magical might, while also defending its remaining territory from Arutu's incursions.

Despite its current challenges, Eplem remains a land of great potential. Its people are resilient and resourceful, and its magic is still a force to be reckoned with. With time, Eplem may yet rise from the ashes of war and reclaim its place as a beacon of culture and power in the realm of Abrigar.
</div>

  ## Emblem

  Eplem's Emblem is designed to reflect their core values and traits, with emphasis on color and ties to Minera. Purple, the snake and sides, represents magic, both at the core and sides of the emblem. Gold, as in Minera's emblem, represents wealth and power. This gold has taken an ironic flip after the war, now representing what was lost when Arutu invaded.

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Eplem is a land of stark contrasts, where lush forests and fertile plains and deep, treacherous valleys. The western border of the realm is marked by War

  The eastern portion of Eplem is a patchwork of rolling hills and fertile plains, crisscrossed by rivers and dotted with charming villages and bustling cities. The land is rich and abundant, producing bountiful crops and supporting a thriving population.

  However, the recent conflict with Arutu has left its mark on Eplem's landscape. The western region, once a vibrant tapestry of forests and fields, now bears the scars of war. The once-verdant forests are scarred and blackened, the cities reduced to rubble, and the air heavy with the lingering scent of smoke and ash.
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Eplem was established in the early days of Abrigar, one of the first nations to rise from the chaotic dawn of civilization. Its formation was not the result of conquest or grand design, but rather a natural evolution—a settlement that gradually took root in fertile lands and grew into something enduring. Although the identities of those who first settled the region have long since faded into obscurity, their influence lingers like a half-remembered dream. Their names may be lost to time, their stories buried beneath centuries of dust, but their presence can still be felt in the customs, ruins, and spirit of Eplem, their legacy cloaked in shadow.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  The government of Eplem is a unique and fascinating entity, governed by a small but powerful council of mages known as the Conclave of Lumina. This council, composed of the most esteemed and respected mages within the realm, holds the ultimate authority over all matters of state.

  The Conclave is not merely a political body; it is a testament to the profound influence of magic within Eplem. Each member of the Conclave is not only a powerful mage but also a scholar, diplomat, and strategist. Their decisions are guided by a deep understanding of magic, a keen awareness of the political landscape, and a commitment to the well-being of their people.

  The Conclave's powers are vast and far-reaching. They wield significant influence over the military, the economy, and the legal system. Their decrees are considered law, and their decisions are rarely challenged. However, the Conclave's power is not absolute. They are bound by a strict code of ethics and a deep respect for the traditions and values of Eplem.
  </div>

## International Relations

Eplem used to be a huge social hub, spreading new ideas, new magics, and progressive thinking. This forward progress put Eplem at the forefront with Minera and Fratera as far as social dominance goes, but the war against Arutu changed everything. Eplem survived, but it took an extreme amount of their resources. The nation is tired now, a shell of what it was before, and its relations have changed because of that. Minera and Fratera now look down on Eplem, as Arutu does, and its status as a powerful nation is  now laughable. Neither Minera nor Fratera came to Eplem's aid in the war, planting a bitter grudge in the hearts of Eplem citizens. 
</div>

  ## Capital - Lumina

  The capital of Eplem is a grand city known as "Lumina," a name that reflects its reputation as a beacon of magical and scholarly pursuits. It stands as a testament to the nation's rich history and cultural influence, even after the devastating conflict with Arutu. 

  Lumina is not only a center of magical learning but also a hub of trade and commerce. Merchants from across Abrigar and beyond flock to the city's bustling markets, seeking rare magical artifacts, exotic goods, and the wisdom of Eplem's scholars. To bolster the agricultural economy of both the capital and the nation at large, the Eplemian government has used earth magic to create many floating islands of stone above the city. These islands are full of farmland lifted high over the roads 

  Despite the recent conflict, Lumina remains a beacon of culture and sophistication. The city is home to renowned theaters, museums, and festivals that celebrate the arts, music, and literature. The streets are filled with the sounds of musicians, poets, and scholars, creating a vibrant and dynamic atmosphere.
</div>

  ## Portshil

  Portshil is a quiet port town, serving as Eplem's main connection to the Shattered Sea. Known for its fairly central position, it could have served as a huge trading hub, had Dorado not claimed that title with such dominance. It's mostly full of Eplemian and Kulcinian traders, facilitating good relations between their countries. Portshil survived the War of Eclipsing Fire fairly unscathed, which has left its inhabitants peaceful, even welcoming.
