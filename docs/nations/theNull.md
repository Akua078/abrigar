<div className="intro">
The Null Wastes, or 'The Wastes', or 'The Null', goes by many names, but each one holds its own infamy. This is a desert nation of warriors and outcasts, protected and occupied by vigilantes such as The Maddocks. This nation is a desert, covered in sand, despite its northern position and being surrounded by rivers. It is said that in ancient history, a curse was placed over the land, making it forever warm, but no evidence has been found of this. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  The Emblem of the Null Wastes was originally designed as a symbol to rally behind during their rebellion, and stuck in the hearts of their patriotic people. The Orange and yellow body are representative of the sands of the wastes and the heat that the Visian Government always resented. The purple flourish represents the magical prowess that led to the Wastes' freedom, and the beacon of freedom and hope that the wastes serve as today. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  The Null Wastes is a giant desert in the middle of Visia. Only a minor spit of land reaches the ocean, providing the only relief from the eternal blockade for the wastes. A river, The Creton River, provides  water travel through the deeper desert, though its mouth into the ocean is heavily fortified, and passage is controlled by Visia, who does not allow any 'rebel' ships through. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  The Null Wastes used to be a part of Visia, although relations were never great. The informal rule of the Maddocks in Anh created political tension, and the difference in culture between the refugees fleeing to the hot desert and the sophisticated nobles in the capital did not bode well. This schism eventually caused a split, and the war between the two nations. The war has been given no formal name, as Visia still insists that the Wastes are simply an active rebellion, despite the fact that the borders haven't moved in almost a century, and the Wastes are entirely self-sufficient. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  The Wastes has a rather non-traditional government. Most call it anarchy, but it is much more organized than most outsiders understand. Laws exist, and are enforced via vigilante justice. Though chaotic and violent, a method exists within the madness. 
  </div>

<div>
## International Relations

The Wastes have odd relations with the other nations. Most don't recognize The Wastes as a nation properly, though engage in trade as if it were. This odd limbo is mostly to appease Visia, and to keep the resources unique to the desert available to the world. Despite these efforts, the goods here are difficult to find on the worldwide market. Visia has held a firm blockade around the desert for decades, insisting the wastes are nothing but an illegitimate rebellion that needs to be destroyed, rather than the proper nation the sands have become. 
</div>
<div className="c2">
<div>
## Travel

Travel through the sands of the wastes is essential, and few creatures can survive the intense heat without preparation. To solve this, the inhabitants ride Dune Foxes, bred to be ridden across the sands. Their large pads allow them to stay afloat on the hot dunes, and their large ears allow them to dissipate heat. Unlike their smaller cousins, these foxes are large creatures, big enough to carry up to three creatures, or two and supplies. These foxes eat the red prickle pears, and often are trained to run great distances on little nourishment. Foxes are hyperactive, eager to run and carry their riders, similar to Balbian sled dogs.

  ## Capital - Anh

  Anh is a city of sandstone, carved bricks from the massive quarries surrounding the city. The place is swarming with open air markets, stall merchants selling goods to travelers that can't be found anywhere else, such as the famous Red Prickle Pears. 
</div>

<div>
  
  ## Saacili

  Saacili is known as the Dune Fox capital. Dune foxes originated near here in the wastes, and wild packs can still be found. Fox Breeders can be found in swathes here, and the most affordable fox lines as a result. Red Prickle Pear fruit stalls line the streets, as well as taverns for travelers. Yuan-Ti often stay here, fleeing persecution from the rest of Abrigar. This race of half snake humanoids often mingle with Lamias, and even, sometimes, Couatls in disguise. This racial acceptance makes Saacili a place of refuge for many persecuted groups, a sanctuary with opportunity.

  ## Saduh Tani

  Saduh Tani is a small fishing town at the northern end of The Wastes. This town acts as the only connection between the sea and The Null, and as such is a critical economic point for the area. In the Red Age, Visia pummeled this town specifically, targeting the ports and merchants, to try and economically cripple the rebellion. Because of this, the town is still highly damaged, and the adjacent waters filled with shipwrecks that could give Broken Mast Bay a run for its money.
