<div className="intro">
Uxbrid is a small island nation that sits west of Anaxin, across many scattered islands and islets. Uxbrid itself is one of the oldest nations in Abrigar, despite being so far from the mainland. Every island in Uxbrid's waters is dotted with ancient structures. Some are little more than weathered statues or collapsed walls, while others are sprawling citadels and entire forgotten towns. Today, these ruins are inhabited by people who had no hand in their construction.

  ## Emblem

  The Uxbrid Emblem is older than most anyone remembers. Archivists have cataloged its appearance in even the oldest ruins, stained glass windows and sherds, all over Uxbrid. Its age is its greatest asset, and also its greatest curse. It is almost unanimously beloved among the people of Uxbrid, but its original meaning has been lost. The black diamond in the center of the emblem in particular is a point of mystery, argued over by archivists and citizens alike.

  <div>
  ## The Ruins

  Uxbrid is littered with the ruins of ancient civilizations from times long before recorded history. These ruins are not only loved, but lived in, as well. Uxbridians have built their homes inside of ancient ruins, often refurbishing and mending the crumbling walls, making homes out of what were once shops or towns. The people who built these structures are long gone, having lived and died long ago, but their construction lives on to carry their memory. Those living in Modern Uxbrid have deep respect for the ruins they live among and the architects whose names were forgotten, and often spend time to maintain and cherish these ancient ruins. The civilization that built these structures is commonly referred to by historians as Ancient Uxbrid, while the people who inhabit the islands today are collectively known as Modern Uxbrid. Whether the ancient civilization ever used that name themselves has long since been forgotten. 
  
  The people of Modern Uxbrid are resourceful because of these ruins, and respecting the ancient stone is the foundation of their culture. Stone Masonry is treated as a holy craft, and only the most proficient are allowed to construct upon ruins beyond standard maintenance. Masons swear oaths to uphold the ruins upon being given the honor to do so, and every repair is documented, such that what is original and what is restored may never be lost again. As such, most Modern Uxbridian homes are wooden, with stone walls for a base. The people of Uxbrid ensure that only the highest respect is given to these stones. Painting over stone is considered deeply disrespectful, children are forbidden from carving into them, only deeply meaningful events are held in them, and construction is prevented from demolishing them. To a Uxbridian, repairing an ancient wall is not altering history, but ensuring it survives another generation.
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Construction and Archivists

  Because so much of Uxbrid is built upon, around, and within ancient structures, no two buildings are ever truly alike. A humble wooden home may share a wall with a palace thousands of years older than the nation itself. One window may be newly built with wooden muntins, while the next is an ancient stained-glass mosaic depicting a forgotten god. To outsiders, the architecture appears chaotic. To the people of Uxbrid, every mismatch is another memory preserved. Uxbridian Archivists use these layers of construction to stitch together what history they can. One carved lintel could confirm a long-held theory, while an uncovered mosaic might overturn centuries of accepted understanding. Every renovation is preceded by careful study, for no craftsman wishes to erase a chapter of history unknowingly.

  ## Government 

  Uxbrid’s Government is non-existent, or at least informal. The Elders of Uxbrid, those archivists or masons who have served the longest and known the most about their nation and its history, are treated with respect. They use this respect as authority to settle disputes, spats, and disagreements. On a large scale, this would be impractical, but Uxbrid has only a sparse and relatively tame population.

## International Relations

Uxbrid sits far away from most other nations. It has little material wealth, no mines or farms, relying on fishing and trade to sustain its people. As such, it has participated in very little international conflict, and known very few enemies. It remains neutral in the affairs of the Shattered Sea, especially the Feuds, and so is trade partners with the other island nations equally.

  ## Capital - The Citadel of Stone

  Uxbrid’s capital city is a large collection of ruins in a coastal town of their largest island. The centerpiece of the town is a large citadel, a huge market district with a large arena in its center. It began from many stone masons gathering here, flocking to the ruins. After the masons, the elders followed, then archivists, creating a natural meeting point for many of the nation’s greatest experts. As such, the arena market is constantly teeming with archivists debating, masons restoring, and elders keeping the peace.
