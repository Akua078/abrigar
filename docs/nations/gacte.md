<div className="intro">
Gacte is a large island nation that holds the largest island in the Shattered Sea, Anaxin. Their nation is dominated by the Anaxin jungle, a wet mess of plant life that would make even the most experienced druid salivate. Roads must be cleared daily, lest the jungle reclaim them overnight. Buildings are raised on stone foundations to protect them from creeping roots and seasonal floods. Every village employs gardeners whose work is as vital as that of carpenters. Their soil is rich in nutrients, and their people are expert farmers and botanists. As such, they produce the vast majority of crops for Abrigar, using massive fields naturally irrigated by the rainfall on the island. If it can be grown, Gacte grows it. From grain and fruit to rare spices, medicinal herbs, and potent poisons, no nation rivals the bounty of Anaxin. Their history, though, is darker than their peaceful present. They emerged as the defeated power in the Koralian Feuds, and they were punished severely for their loss, now forced to be the only island nation with no navy of any kind. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  Gacte’s emblem was designed well after the Feuds, by the first generation to move on from its anger and bitterness. The pink flower in the center is an Anaxin Jungle Rose, a flower native only to Anaxin, and the symbol of the nation’s new peace. The green behind it represents the jungle of the nation, and the fertility of the soil that now sustains the nation. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Gacte was reduced to having no navy after the feuds, but its claim to several islands near Anaxin were not changed. This was an intentional move on Koral’s part, forcing those islands to rot and their villages to become abandoned. A few villages survived on their own, forming their own identity separate from Gacte, but most died out or moved away. The islands that ran empty are said to be now choked with monsters and creatures feeding off the fertile soil themselves.  
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Gacte lost the Koralian Feuds, a crushing defeat by Koral and Kulcin. After the feuds were over, the winners instated harsh terms on Gacte, forbidding them from having a single military ship, any ship construction, or the like, ensuring complete dependence on Koralian trade. As such, there is one port on the northern side of the island, a bay belonging to Koral, where the people of Gacte are allowed to trade. Now, Gacte is subdued, but its people found peace in the jungle, and in growing food for the world. Despite their lack of a naval fleet, they dominate the global food trade. The people of Gacte often joke that they do not cultivate the jungle. They merely convince it to grow where they ask. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Gacte’s government is a constitutional monarchy. A Queen serves as a figurehead, where an elected body of representatives make up a small parliament. These officials serve long terms, and the title of queen is passed down by bloodline. The government is intentionally peaceable, dealing primarily with trade, agriculture, and diplomacy. Most day-to-day affairs are left to local communities, allowing Gacte's many cultures to coexist with little interference. Their restrained approach to governance is intentional, encouraging cooperation over conflict throughout the nation.  Unlike many nations that speak of peace while preparing for war, Gacte has built its identity around lasting prosperity rather than military strength. Their people respect the government for letting them rest after the war, and the unexpected peace filled with prosperity has soothed the anger of many of the people. The Queen especially, and her family the Everblooms, are well liked for their kindness and generosity. 
  </div>

## International Relations

Gacte, despite losing the Koralian Feuds badly, has had hundreds of years to heal and move on. Some ancient veterans of races that can live so long are still bitter, but most of the new generations quietly move forward. Gacte has since served as a refuge for those seeking peace from conflict, freedom from tension, and relief from pain. They have a good relationship with the Whispering Woods, seeing as many druids from the jungle of Anaxin share close ties with the druids of the woods, and Visia, for similar reasons. 
</div>

  ## Capital - Kirton

  Kirton is the capital of Gacte, and was once its pride and joy. It sits in the center of the Bay of Anaxin, an inlet with an island in its center. Kirton stands on that island, which it once did proudly, now, like a cruel joke. The water is too deep to be crossed without a ship, and now, no ships are allowed in Gacte. This forces every Gactan citizen who wishes to visit the capital to take a Koralian ship, and be subject to its fares. As such, Kirton has plummeted in economic importance in the region, and only the government of the nation ever really visits the island, and they usually choose to live there. 
</div>

<div>
  
  ## Murti

  Murti is the main port city of Gacte. It is located on the northern shore of Anaxin, it is technically Koralian soil. Ships sail in and out of here so quickly there are triton mages hired specifically to guide ships in and out of the harbor without choking up the entrance, and enough docks to hold a truly enormous amount of vessels. This is all to facilitate one of the largest food trades in the world.
