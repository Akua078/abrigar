<div className="intro">
Kulcin is an island nation to the far east of Abrigar. What it lacks in raw materials and trading power, it makes up for in magic. It is known as the home of storm sorcerers, and of many magical secrets. Powerful mages find their homes here, protected by the storms and wards of the main island, and flock here to study their craft in peace.

Their national identity is firm, their confidence unshakable. Lightning is their symbol of national identity, how they won the Koralian Feuds, and the strength they still hold. They're flashy, some would say arrogant, but powerful regardless. 
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  The emblem of Kulcin is a pale blue star surrounded by streaks of grey and light grey, which represents the lightning they surround themselves with. It is often worn on flags of their ships, on banners of towers and lighthouses, and shields of their soldiers, to show off national pride. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Kulcin is an archipelligo nation, ruling in the northwestern corner of the Shattered Sea. The main island, the biggest in Kulcin, is Marso. It is shrouded in rain at all times, and depending on the situation, it can be anything from a light drizzle to a typhoon, or even a hurricane. Their wizards discovered that creatures soaked by the storm were much more vulnerable to electric spells, and so they turned their entire island into a weapon, and poured resources into maintaining their ritual. Kulcin is constantly rainy because of the Grand Wizard's spell, meaning the people prepare. They often wear clothes made of sea animals such as the native stingrays and seals, waterproof cloaks that keep them warm and dry. They've incorporated this into their fashion, with the greys and blues of the storm making their way into the national identity of Kulcin. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Kulcin emerged from the Koralian Feuds with control of most of the islands in the western half of the Shattered Sea. They won Marso, a highly contested territory, through a combination of Eplem's support and the might of their own storm mages. It is said that during the battle which sealed Marso's fate, the first Grand Wizard conjured the storm that still shrouds the island today, empowering Kulcin's sorcerers and forever changing the land.

  Those storm sorcerers became legends, drawing countless like-minded mages to Kulcin's banner. The concentration of magical talent transformed the nation into an overwhelming military power, ultimately securing a decisive victory in the Koralian Feuds. In recognition of their service to Koral, Kulcin was granted uncontested rule over the northwestern archipelago and a voice in deciding the fate of the defeated nations.

  Though the war ended centuries ago, no Grand Wizard has ever allowed the storm to cease. It has become the defining symbol of Kulcin, protecting its people, empowering its magic, and reminding the world how Marso was won. 
  </div>

  ## Government 

  Kulcin is ruled by a grand wizard, who lives in the tower at the center of Vendeval. The Grand Wizard is in charge of maintaining the storm that engulfs Marso.

## International Relations

Kulcin is known to have a very good relationship with Eplem, and is rumored to even have sent aid in their war against Arutu. This is because the royal families of Eplem and Kulcin are interwoven, related though not directly. In the past, during the Koralian Feuds, Eplem lent its own aid to Kulcin, which is said to have begun their good relationship. 
</div>

  ## Capital - Vendeval

  Vendeval is known as the rainy city, tucked deep in an inlet on Marso, it serves as the capital of Kulcin. The Grand Wizard lives here in a massive tower, enacts laws, enforces them with a police force of scrying mages, and maintains the spell to keep the island in the rain.

  The Grand Tower of Vendeval, as it is called, is where the center of Marso's storm is, and its source. A massive, ongoing ritual is the centerpiece of the tower, resembling a massive blue crystal, swirling with magic and shooting a beam of blue light into the sky. This beam causes the storm, putting water into the sky, and the Grand Wizard keeps the ritual going.
