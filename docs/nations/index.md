<u> 
# The Nations of Abrigar
</u>

Abrigar is a world full of people, politics, peace, and warfare. 19 nations lay claim to Abrigar's land, and their interactions and relationships are the foundational context of the lives of their citizens, the nature of their cultures, and the development of their stories. These are the nations of Abrigar, from the richest to the poorest, the most influential to the most remote.
