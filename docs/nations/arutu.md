<div className="intro">
Arutu is a region defined by its sprawling countryside, a tapestry of rolling fields and fertile farmland that stretches as far as the eye can see. The air is thick with the scent of freshly turned earth and ripening crops, a testament to the region's agricultural heart.

In the northern reaches of Arutu, two large lakes Ironscale and Silver Shoal Lake, dominate the landscape. Here, vibrant fishing communities thrive, their lives intricately tied to the rhythms of the water. Nets are cast at dawn, and the harbors buzz with the activity of seasoned fishermen returning with their daily catch.

However, beneath this veneer of pastoral tranquility lies the firm hand of military governance. General Thrain Blackheart's influence permeates every aspect of Arutu's society. Garrisons stand watch over key settlements, and the presence of soldiers is a constant reminder of the region's controlled environment. The capital city, Regiment City, serves as the nerve center of this military power, its imposing structures a symbol of unwavering authority.
</div>

  <div>
  ## Emblem

  The Emblem of Arutu, made by General Thrain Blackheart, the first general of Arutu, represents the mindset of the nation. The black gauntlet represents the Blackheart Family, ruling the land with an iron fist. The gold flowing from the gauntlet represents the prosperity their rule brings, and the purple surrounding it represents the darkness of those who are foolish enough to resist.
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Geography

  Arutu is located in the Midwest of Abrigar, bordering Eplem to its Western side. Three Lakes, Lake Ironscale, Silver Shoal Lake, and Venom Lake are controlled by Arutu, and boats are used as a fairly common means of transportation across them, traveling along the Arutuan River. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Origins

  Arutu emerged naturally, as one of the first nations in Abrigar. When these first few nations formed, the people of Abrigar didn't write much down, so most history from this time period mostly exists in oral history. People say that Arutu was formed after a great war, where they were the sole survivors of a horrific apocalypse, a testament to Arutu's strength. These rumors are yet to be proven, and no evidence exists of a great cataclysm, but many elders hold fast to this idea.

  Arutu and Eplem have been at odds since their conception, and lately, Arutu has been coming out on top. 
  </div>

  <div>
  ## Government 

  Arutu is run by a militant government lead by the general of the nation and managed by his 4 captains who rule the different regions in the nation. These four captains each rule a province of Arutu, after being chosen by The General and report directly to him, following his orders and laws.

  The Captains have a fair amount of autonomy in their provinces, though The General still maintains control of the Captains themselves. The General controls the Province of Kalb, where Regiment City and Blackheart Manor reside, and oversees the government of the nation as a whole, as well as planning and running wars, usually against Eplem.
  </div>

## International Relations

Arutu’s international relations are defined by simmering hostility, strategic isolation, and thinly veiled suspicion. Its aggressive annexation of Eplem territory has not only ignited a bitter standoff with Eplem, complete with militarized borders and frozen diplomacy, but has also rattled the rest of Abrigar. Neighboring nations now regard Arutu as a volatile power, wary of its ambitions and hesitant to engage beyond cautious formalities. Trade has dwindled, alliances have cooled, and Arutu stands increasingly alone—armed, unrepentant, and viewed as a growing threat in an already fragile region. 

The new regime, though, under General Dorian Blackheart, seems to be cooling down the tension, bringing a surprising lack of hostility from the militaristic nation. 
</div>

  ## Capital - Regiment City

  Regiment City, the capital and undisputed central hub of Arutu, stands as a formidable testament to the military might and unwavering control of General Thrain Blackheart. Unlike the more organically grown settlements scattered across the countryside, Regiment City was deliberately forged as a symbol of authority and a highly efficient engine of governance. Its very layout and architecture speak of order, discipline, and the omnipresent influence of the military.

  Regiment City is characterized by its rigid, almost geometric design. Broad, straight avenues intersect at precise angles, forming neat blocks that house barracks, administrative buildings, and tightly packed residences for those serving the military and the state. There's a distinct lack of the winding alleys and haphazard growth found in less controlled settlements. Everything in Regiment City feels deliberate, planned, and subject to regulation.

  ## The Twilight Arena

  The Twilight Arena is a massive, open-air amphitheater carved into the side of a mountain, its stone walls rising high above the surrounding landscape. The arena floor is a vast expanse of packed earth, ringed by rows of tiered seating that rise steeply towards the top of the mountain. Thousands of spectators can cram into the arena, their voices echoing off the stone walls as they cheer on their favorite gladiators.

  The arena is the centerpiece of a sprawling complex that includes barracks for the gladiators, training grounds, and a variety of shops and stalls selling food, drink, and souvenirs. The complex is always bustling with activity, as gladiators train for their next fight, spectators place bets on the upcoming matches, and merchants hawk their wares.

  The Twilight Arena is more than just a place of entertainment, however. It is also a place where justice is meted out. When two individuals or factions have a dispute that cannot be resolved peacefully, they may be summoned to the arena to settle their differences in a gladiatorial combat. The victor is declared the rightful winner, and their opponent is either killed or enslaved.

  The Twilight Arena is a brutal and bloody place, but it is also a place of great excitement and spectacle. The gladiators are skilled warriors, and their fights are often thrilling to watch. The crowds are always eager to see blood and gore, and they roar their approval as the combatants clash in the arena.

  The Twilight Arena is a symbol of the power and brutality of Arutu. It is a place where the strong prey on the weak, and where the pursuit of entertainment can often lead to death and destruction.
</div>

<div>
  ## Blackheart Manor

  Blackheart Manor is a sprawling, gothic-inspired mansion nestled amidst a grove of ancient oaks, a short distance north of the bustling capital city of Regiment City. It stands as a stark contrast to the rigid, regimented architecture of the city, its design a reflection of General Thrain Blackheart's own personality – imposing, brooding, and steeped in a sense of dark elegance.

  The manor is constructed from dark, weathered stone, its walls rising high and crowned with a series of centralized towers that hint at its defensive capabilities. Narrow, slit-like windows cast long, ominous shadows across the meticulously manicured grounds, where clipped hedges form intricate, geometric patterns. A long, gravel driveway winds its way up to the imposing double doors, flanked by two stone gargoyles perched menacingly.

  Inside, the manor is a labyrinth of opulent chambers, each adorned with rich tapestries, dark wood paneling, and furnishings that hint at a life of wealth and power. The air is thick with the scent of expensive woods, polished metals, and something faintly acrid – a lingering hint of the gunpowder used to forge the General's reputation.

  The manor is not just a residence; it is a symbol of Blackheart's authority and a testament to his wealth and influence. It is a place where he retreats to escape the pressures of governing Arutu, a place where he can indulge in his darker inclinations and plot his next moves.

  In the heart of the manor lies the General's private study, a room draped in heavy velvet curtains and filled with maps, weapons, and artifacts collected from his many campaigns. Here, Blackheart spends countless hours poring over intelligence reports, plotting military strategies, and indulging in his penchant for cruelty and intrigue.

  Blackheart Manor is a place of secrets and shadows, a place where the lines between personal indulgence and political maneuvering blur. It is a fitting home for the man who rules Arutu with an iron fist, a testament to his power, and a constant reminder of the darkness that lurks beneath the surface of his seemingly ordered world.

  ## The Arutuan Lakes
  ![The Arutuan Lakes](/img/nations/arutuLakesMap.png)

<div className="c3">
<div>
  ### Venom Lake

  Easily the biggest lake in Arutu, and all of Abrigar, Venom Lake is a long standing dispute of territory between Arutu and Eplem. The conflict is bitter, considering the importance and size of Venom Lake and its facilitation of trade, being the deepest water route in Abrigar, hence the name. 
</div>
<div>
  ### Silver Shoal Lake

  The smallest Arutuan lake. It lies in between the other two, used mostly as a water route deeper into Arutu, and even into Venom Lake to access The Whispering Woods and Eplem. Fishing is quite the attraction here, the lake being filled to the brim with silver fang carp, a kind of carnivorous fish known for its silver scales. 
</div>
<div>
  ### Lake Ironscale

  Lake Ironscale is the first lake a ship comes to when traveling up the Gnok River System, going west to Arutu. Its known for its strategic position, serving as a hard border between Arutu and Haldaerok. What little navy Haldaerok has is stored here, ready for deployment to Fracture Lake, usually. Riverswatch is settled into the Northwest corner of the Lake, serving as a control point for the river leading to deeper Arutu and beyond. 
</div>
