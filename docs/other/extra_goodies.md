<div>

<div className="c_right">
<div>
This is an early image of the Trash Panda, back before it became a literal panda. They were originally meant to be a pun on the nickname for racoons, but later I decided it would be more horrifying if they were cuddly pandas at first glance. This very big gremlin is what remains of that early concept. 
</div>
<div>![Prototype Trash Panda](/img/extra/prototypeTrashPandaBG.png)</div>
</div>

<div className="c_left">
<div>
<div>![The Red Hungry Prototype](/img/extra/prototypeHungryRed.png)</div>
</div>
Similarly, this is an early design of The Red Hungry, from The Hungry Ones. This was the first image I was decently happy with, though the final design changed to match his friends. The original idea was much more vicious than what came out, when Red was meant to do most of the damage, using those massive teeth to maul his victims, and those long legs to sprint down halls like a rabid wolf. 
</div>

<div className="c_right">
<div>
This is an early image generated for the skiff fox. The final image didn't change much, but I like this image enough to not throw it away completely. It better reflects the more innocent nature of the skiff fox concept, being a mischevious pest rather than a cunning thief. 
</div>
<div>![Prototype Trash Panda](/img/extra/prototypeSkiffFox.png)</div>
</div>
