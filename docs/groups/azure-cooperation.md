# Azure Cooperation

The Azure Cooperation is a powerful political and economic alliance spanning numerous islands throughout Abrigar.

> "Nothing is beyond our reach."

## Overview

The Azure Cooperation maintains a vast network of ports, trade routes, diplomatic agreements, and transportation infrastructure.

Its influence extends far beyond the territories it directly controls.

## Government

The Cooperation is governed through a network of representatives from its member territories.

Although individual islands retain varying degrees of autonomy, the Cooperation maintains significant authority over:

- Trade
- Transportation
- Foreign relations
- Maritime security
- Portal infrastructure

## Military

The Azure Cooperation maintains one of the largest organized naval forces in Abrigar.

Its ships patrol major trade routes and protect Cooperation territory from pirates and hostile powers.

## Portal Network

One of the Cooperation's greatest achievements is its network of portals.

These portals allow people and goods to travel enormous distances almost instantaneously.

The system is summarized by the Cooperation's famous motto:

> "Nothing is beyond our reach."

## Relations

The Cooperation maintains diplomatic relationships with numerous nations and organizations throughout Abrigar.

Its relationships with independent pirate crews are considerably more complicated.
