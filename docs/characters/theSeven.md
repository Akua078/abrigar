# The Seven
[First appeared in 'Swords of Abrigar']

The Seven was a legendary adventuring party that wandered Abrigar long ago, around the late 300s PC to the early 400s PC. They defeated many great monsters and rescued their fair share of damsels, but their origins, their disappearance, and their motives are still shrouded in mystery. Rumors even say that some members of the Seven are still alive, though the presence of their companion Iapyx is known. They wandered Abrigar, taking on the quarrels of many villages and hearing the pleas of the poor, and helped them. As a result, their deeds became legendary, and their names became known around Abrigar as heroes of the weak, saviors of the helpless. Why they traveled together, their plan and purpose, remains a mystery to the public. Iapyx, their leader's sentient armor, remains, but refuses to speak on what happened to his compatriots exactly. All he reveals is that Marlamin Arulo betrayed the group, and left only Iapyx alive. He took vengeance, but no one knows more.

## Michael Maluna

<figure>
        ![Micheal Maluna](/img/characters/michaelMaluna.png)
</figure> 

<div className="tiny"> Human Artificer </div>
Michael Maluna formed The Seven, along with his brother Elijah. He was known as a legendary artificer from the city of Fragua, Minera. He forged many great works, his greatest being his own  sentient armor, named Iapyx.

He was known for having a general disregard for the safety of others and a lack of enthusiasm for saving the innocents his troupe became known for. His brother, though, convinced him to work with the group to go on these heroic adventures, acting as a counterweight in the team.
</div>

<div>
## Elijah Maluna

<figure>
        ![Elijah Maluna](/img/characters/elyMaluna.png)
</figure> 

<div className="tiny"> Human Sorcerer / Wizard </div>
Elijah Maluna formed The Seven, along with his brother Michael. He was known as a legendary mage and wizard from the city of Stellae, Fratera.  He outshone many great wizards, and spent his time diving deep into hundreds of topics, learning so quickly as to infuriate wizards who spent their entire lives studying a subject only to be overcome in a matter of weeks.

Elijah acted naively, but with heart, acting as the emotional center of the group and keeping his brother grounded. He frequently got the group into dangerous situations, but even more often saved their lives from danger with his extreme magical proficiency.
</div>

<div>
## Marlamin Arulo

<figure>
        ![Marlamin Arulo](/img/characters/marlaminArulo.png)
</figure> 

<div className="tiny"> Human Artificer </div>
Marlamin Arulo was the first to join The Seven, seeking glory and reputation for himself. He was known as a fantastic wizard, second only to Elijah Maluna himself, and also from Stellae, Fratera. He studied hard to learn many fields of arcana, proficient in a great many spells, but specializing in conjuration, specifically teleportation.

Marlamin was hundreds of years old, ancient, even for an elf. His exact age is unknown, as he typically dodges the question. Marlamin was known for being unpredictable, but often very wise. He was often described as 'cooky' or 'wild', but his magics saved The Seven several times.
</div>
