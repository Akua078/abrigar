# Duquru Timmix

<div>
    Duquru Timmix was a performer from Lumina, the capital city of Eplem known for its magic. Duquru is a half-elf, but his parents abandoned him for an unknown reason when he was an infant. Perhaps they could not care for him. Perhaps he is the result of some scandal or drama. He can't know. He was found, crying in an alley, by a kindly old tabaxi woman, named Amabel Timmix. He has always just called her Mammy. Duquru was raised like a tabaxi kitten, and enjoys the closeness that comes with the dynamic. This made him an unusual child in the schools of Lumina, but that didn't stop him. Duquru loved his unique walk of life, and came to enjoy the attention. Duquru found his love for performance, and particularly, sword dancing.

    Duquru was determined to become a sword dancer in Lumina, to perform on the streets and in theatres as much as he could, and when he began, he was adored. He was known as the 'young sword dancer in red', or 'the purple haired sword dancer'. He spread his reputation, and soon, when he danced, he was known by name. His outfit was a bold, bright red, tailored and handcrafted by Mammy herself, and he dyed his hair purple. His colors were unmistakable, his brand and his passion undeniable, and he loved it. He was on the fast track to become a fantastic sword dancer. But Duquru made one fatal mistake. He wanted more. He liked the performance, but he also wanted to be seen as strong. Duquru wanted to know how to use that sword for more than dancing, but he didn't know how. So he cheated. He asked a fey trickster to make him not just a great sword dancer, but a great sword fighter as well. 

    The trickster agreed to his deal, and assured him that before the year was out, he would have been known as the greatest sword fighter in Lumina. In exchange, Duquru traded part of his soul to the fey. But, as most deals with the fey, it was a trick. The fey ensured that Duquru became incredible at sword fighting, and by the end of the year, he would have been known as the best sword fighter that ever "was", and cursed Duquru to die within the year. 

    In an attempt to thwart this deal, Duquru ran away from Lumina, the only home he had ever known. If he could not be recognized, he reasoned, he could not become a famous sword fighter within the year, and thus the deal would be off. Duquru fled until he found comfort in Orro Elasi, an adventurer and a member of the coveted Vanguard. Eventually, the architect of the Vanguard took pity on Duquru, and allowed him to join the group. In exchange, Duquru was given his mask, a large, white mask that completely concealed his face. In this way, Duquru was enabled to use his sword fighting power, and dance to his hearts content. But never again could he be known. Never again could Duquru have the attention he craved be attached to him, or his face. He would be revered, but never as himself, only as the masked sword fighter he was cursed to be.
