# The Maddocks
[First appeared in 'Legacy of Shadow']

The Maddocks are a clan of bounty hunters who have unofficially laid claim to the Null Wastes. They are a feared group, known as elite hunters who never miss a target they set their eyes on. Their family of the same name has existed for hundreds of years, though their exact origins are shrouded in mystery. A familiarity with the Iron Sword and a distaste for vigilante justice outside their own speaks volumes, however, about their similarly organized rivals. 

The Maddocks themselves are elite warriors, either trained from birth or welcomed in via an extensive and secretive initiation process, but their weaponry is what sets them truly apart. They hold a cache of weapons unlike anything else seen in Abrigar. From Antimatter Rifles to Pistols, they call these weapons 'Arcane Arcubi' (Arcane Arcubus for singular). They can dish out some serious damage, but only if you know how to use them. 

The Maddocks create these weapons, but the process is heavily guarded, and seems to take a very, very long time. As well, repairing an item like these is nearly impossible without complete reconstruction, it seems. 

The Maddocks believe in justice and honor above all, including law. Most governments have it out for them, especially those in Visia, but none have the strength to do much about it. Their weapons put them above most others, but their secrets prove a fatal weakness.

## Catastrophe Maddock

Catastrophe “Cata” or “Cass” Maddock is a fiercely independent, sharp-tongued bounty hunter with a ruthless streak and a heart she fiercely guards. Recognizable by her long, curly purple hair, hazel eyes, and leather-clad appearance, she is a skilled combatant, artillery expert, and calculating strategist who dispatches her targets with deadly precision. Quick-tempered and armed with a sarcastic wit, she uses flattery to get what she wants and insults to keep others at a distance. Beneath her hardened exterior, however, lies a deeply loyal and protective woman whose family and clan mean everything to her. She prefers solitude but finds companionship in her trusted companion, her immortal skeletal dire wolf Hades. Away from the hunt, she tinkers with her weapons and contemplates the mysteries of the universe and the nature of good and evil.

<div>
### Nyx Maddock

Hale and Justice’s daughter, second-in-command of the clan, and smarter than you by at least two lifetimes. Strategic, unshakable, and perpetually unimpressed. She’s the kind of person who can end a man’s career with a raised eyebrow. If Knight is the fist and Flash the hammer, Nyx is the blade sliding between the ribs. 
</div>
<div>
### Great Granny Morwen Maddock

The iron-willed matriarch and oldest living Maddock at 102. She’s seen more battles, buried more enemies, and dealt more damage than anyone else alive. Half the clan swears she’s immortal. Still drinks whiskey straight, still trains the young members, still terrifying. Cassie inherited her soul from this woman — no doubt. 
</div>
<div>
### Flash Maddock

The middle brother and the Maddock Clan’s Enforcer. He’s the security head, blunt instrument, and human sledgehammer all in one. Where Knight is a scalpel, Flash is a battering ram. Doesn’t do subtle. Doesn’t need to. Raised Cassie like a pitbull with a heart — will tease her endlessly, but gods help the poor soul who actually crosses her. 
</div>
<div>
### Knight Maddock

The eldest sibling and the clan’s reigning bounty hunter champ (with more confirmed kills than most entire adventuring parties), Knight trained Cassie himself. Quietly intense, terrifyingly efficient, and battle-scarred from years of hunting multiplanar scum, he’s the kind of guy who kills you with your own weapon just to prove a point — then goes home and reads philosophy. Second only to his father (the late Judge Maddock) in legendary status. 
</div>
<div>
### Justice and Hale Maddock

The current heads of the Maddock Clan. Justice is Cassie's dad’s older brother, and Hale is the steely-eyed tactician married to him. Together, they're the backbone of the clan — seasoned, ruthless, honorable in their own grim way. They uphold the clan's code with an iron will and love their family like it’s religion. 
</div>
<div>
### Judge and Serenity Maddock

Cassie’s late parents. Judge was a born Maddock, the youngest of his siblings, and once the most promising bounty hunter before he and Serenity were murdered by the Shadow Weaver. Serenity was a kind, fierce soul who married into the clan and fit like a blade into a sheath. Their deaths are the emotional wound that fuels Cassie’s relentless drive. 
</div>
