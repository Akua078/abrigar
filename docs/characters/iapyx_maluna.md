# Iapyx Maluna

<div>
    In Abrigar, to public knowledge, life is biological. Humanoid creatures are intelligent, usually bipedal, and easily distinguished from golems, elementals, and beasts. Iapyx Maluna is the exception. He was made by Micheal Maluna, a legendary artificer who built wonders, and is the only widely known artificial life form. Iapyx is a man made of metal, a living suit of armor that could be worn, but when doffed, could continue to walk, speak, and fight. Philosophers around the world debate about his sentience, mages marvel at his construction, and Iapyx himself continuously proves that he cannot be ignored. 

    Iapyx served Michael Maluna for decades. Iapyx was his armor, the sentient armor of the most skilled artificer in existence. When Michael died in battle during the fateful fall of the seven, Iapyx lived on. He wandered the world until the Iron Sword found him, and a small civil war began. Those who followed Marlamin Arulo fought against Iapyx, but many loyalists who had once followed Michael now followed Iapyx. Iapyx found himself to be their leader, and spent centuries battling Marlamin. One day, in 572 PC, Iapyx won the war with the support of the recently empowered Dorian Blackheart, and Marlamin was slain. Iapyx swore allegiance to Dorian, and became an Arutuan Capitan, of the Capta territory no less. 

    Now, Iapyx serves as the undisputed leader of the Iron Sword, a mighty arutuan capitan, and a walking philosophical debate. One thing is certain about Iapyx. 
    
    He is a legend in his own right. 
</div>
