# Abrigar

Welcome, wandering Soul, to Abrigar.

This site serves as a living archive of the world of Abrigar. Within these pages you will find its history, cultures, myths, conflicts, and the forces that have shaped it. Think of it as a public record of what is known, studied, and believed within the world itself. 
The information here reflects what a well-read scholar or attentive citizen of Abrigar might reasonably know. Secrets uncovered through specific campaigns or individual characters are treated differently, and are noted as such when applicable. 
All entries are presented in the context of the Year 573 PC, the year 'Legacy of Shadow' and 'Skeleton Crew' begin. 
Not everything recorded here is true, and not every truth is recorded here. This archive represents what is commonly accepted, debated, or rumored amongst the people of Abrigar. Some entries may be incomplete or vague . History is imperfect. Knowledge is evolving. There is always more to uncover. 
Explore freely, and enjoy the show.
--Akua

## Basic Information

It's important to remember that Abrigar is a world wholly unlike our own, filled with different races and ideas, magics and sciences, and that these things affect how culture develops. Below is a summary of information that is too general to be specifically mentioned, and information that indicates a significant difference between our world and Abrigar. Be aware that everything on this page, contrary to my earlier comment, is true and can be trusted to remain so.
