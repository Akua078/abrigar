<div className="intro">
Abrigar is a huge place, full of hundreds of unique foods, spices, tastes and delicacies. Here is a list of the most unique, the most interesting, and the most iconic foods from all corners of the realm, as well as notes on their cultivation, preparation, and history. From the delectable steaks of the most talented butchers, to delicious sweets crafted by anarchous artisans, there is no shortage of variety to be found in Abrigarian cuisine. 
</div>

<div className = "c3">
  <div>
  ![Rock Root](/img/world/rockRoot.png)
  ## "Rock Roots"

  Rock Roots are known for their tough exterior, comparable to stone in durability. They appear as ordinary rocks, but on the inside carry a small amount of nutrients. They grow frequently in The Forbidden Peaks and in the Fraguan Range, and are often the last resort for miners and travelers who have run out of food, though only the desperate go for them.
  </div>
  <div>
  ![Hell Peppers](/img/world/hellPeppers.png)
  ## Red Hell Peppers

  Hell Peppers are known for their spice, grown right in the depths of the underworld. They are too hot to handle for all but the sturdiest of warriors, lethal to the unprepared. Dwarves tend to love the spice, and are one of the few peoples that can handle the heat of this pepper. Tieflings are the only ones really able to give them a reliable run for their gold, though many dwarves claim that the "devils" are cheating with their affinity for fire. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ![Koralian Seamelon](/img/world/seamelon.png)
  ## Koralian Seamelon

  Native to the Akwiyacwiny Atoll, having spread all throughout Koral, Seamelons are large, green melons that grow underwater. When they are ripe, they become buoyant in water and float to the surface. Their inner flesh is a light peach color, and is very sweet. 

  "Very big cranberries." - Empira
  </div>
</div>

<div className = "c2">
  <div>
  ![Manaberries](/img/world/manaberries.png)
  ## Morganan Manaberries

  Morganan Manaberries, or just manaberries, are ironically most commonly found in the Whispering Woods. They are native to Bell's End, said to be a result of the end of the famous general, each berry containing part of the magic used in his defeat. But those are surely just rumors.
  </div>

  <div>
  ![Minerith Maize](/img/world/corn.png)
  ## Minerith Maize

  Minerith Maize is a staple food item in Minera, grown in the northeastern portion of Minera as a food supply. Much is actually eaten by the people as opposed to sold as most nations do with their crop. 
  </div>
</div>

<div className = "c3">
  <div>
  ![Lantern Fruit](/img/world/lanternFruit.png)
  ## Lantern Fruit

  Balbian Lantern Fruits are large, orange fruits that grow in abundance on trees in Balbian greenhouses. They were originally from Farithian Deltas, but were taken en masse and have largely gone extinct on the mainland. Now, they thrive in the lush humidity of the greenhouses, and light the way for travelers to the Ice.
  </div>
  <div>
  ![Prickle Pear](/img/world/redPricklePear.png)
  ## Red Prickle Pears

  Red Prickle Pears are small, spiked pears native to the Null Wastes. They suck to try and open without a blade, but the inside has plenty of water and nourishment for weary travelers. It is said they feed off magic deep in the sands, and thus are not viable to grow outside the wastes. So far, no plantation has disproven this claim. Dune foxes love these things, and they can actually last a long time without spoiling.  
  </div>
  <div>
  ![Squid Sushi](/img/world/squidSushi.png)
  ## Squid Sushi

  Squid Sushi is a dish native to Koral, specifically the Southern Gabbi region. It is made of the sunset squid that are native to the area, and is considered exotic by most of Abrigar, despite being very common in its homeland. 

  "Very big cranberries." - Empira
  </div>
</div>

<div className = "c2">
  <div>
  ![Frateran Cinnamon Roll](/img/world/cinnamonRoll.png)
  ## Frateran Cinnamon Rolls

  Frateran Cinnamon Rolls are some of the most delectable treats in Abrigar. They are made with magic baked right into the dough, and are said to have enough sugar to kill a small mouse. They spoil quickly if not kept in perfect conditions, warm and humid, so be sure to eat quickly!
  </div>

  <div>
  ![Lewen Sweets](/img/world/lewenSweets.png)
  ## Lewen Sweets

  Lewen sweets are a term used to describe all kinds of candies made in Lewe. In the anarchy, and in the complete lack of food safety regulations, these sweets can be made with all kinds of unsafe and addictive magicks, making the purchase of simple candy a roll of the dice. Still, many in Lewe take this chance. Most get a sweet treat, some get a delayed cast fireball for a stomachache. 
  </div>
</div>



<div className = "c3">
  <div>
  ## Arutuan Pike

  Arutuan Pike can be found all throughout the lakes of Arutu, and the rivers connecting them. They are water based predators, often hunting smaller fish and feeding on the weak that stumble into the shallows. They have become a staple of Arutuan cuisine, and are quite popular in the Warcradle Nations as well thanks to their abundance. 
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Ice Eels

  The waters of Ishkari are known for their brisk cold and unforgiving currents. Living in these conditions, though, is an abundance of life to be caught by fishermen. A staple of the Ishkaran diet is the Ice Eel, also known as the Frost Eel. They have long, slick bodies that are freezing to the touch, and can camouflage into ice and rock as needed to hide from predators. They crave the cold, and instead of worms, they are commonly caught using frozen rocks on the end of lures, which they latch onto before being yanked free of their watery home.
  </div>
  <div>
  ## Visian Venison

  Visia, with its lush forests, houses many creatures. The favorite of local hunters is the blink stag, a deer like creature, which makes great stews and other meals. Its become a staple of the area, naturally, along with herbs and spices from the deltas. The difficulty of hunting the blink stags makes this venison a delicacy, highly sought after for its tender and tasty meat.
  </div>
