<div className="intro">
Holidays in Abrigar are days of celebration, dread, or bloodshed. Many resemble holidays in our own world, some are unique. The twelve Months are each twenty eight days long, the seasons split evenly among them. The seasons can be thought of at quarters of the year, and each lasts the same amount of time. That is, the first three months are spring, the second three summer, and so on. Littered throughout those seasons are holidays, celebrating great events in the history of the world or marking the days of great fear, or noting days of significance. In any case, these are the most significant days of the year in Abrigar.
</div>

<div>
## Highsun Solstice

The Highsun Solstice is the summer solstice in Abrigar. On Highsun 28th, the longest, hottest day of the year takes place. Many plants flourish on this day, gathering huge amounts of sunlight before autumn inevitably takes away the warm summer air. 
</div>

<div>
## Icebear Low

In Icebear, the winter solstice of Abrigar hits. The Moon grows as close to Abrigar as it ever does, and many magical aspects of the world begin to react. Aurora Kelp glows as if the Ishkaran Sea were aflame, followers of Bisu are empowered, and phase moths flicker frequently as the border between the ethereal and the material weakens marginally. 
</div>

<div>
## Winter's Giving

Winter's Giving is the poster child of holidays in Abrigar. Spirits of the feywild and celestial beings are much more active, and the borders between planes are weakening before the new year. It's a jolly day full of giving, spirited family, and dear friends. Many of those unprepared for the Hunt say their goodbyes now, spending the last week readying to die. Others spend the time welcoming guests to their homes, readying to shelter and celebrate another year survived and to harbor those unfortunate souls who cannot protect themselves.
</div>

<div>
## Green Moon

The last day of the year was not decided arbitrarily, less of a holiday, more of an annual natural disaster. Every 360 days, the lunar and solar cycles align, and magic grows strongest. The Moon is tainted green by the energy of the event, the weave itself spreading across the land. The fae roam free, a night of terror and dread for the unprepared, but a night of revelry and adrenaline for those who are ready. Many hunt the wild beasts that emerge, many are hunted by spirits both malicious and feral. This night goes by many names, The Fairy Hunt, The Green Moon, The Final Night, and more. One thing is sure. No one is safe tonight, not the material plane, and not the fairies that roam free.
</div>
