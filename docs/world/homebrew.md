<div>
### Amulet of Identify Caster
<div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Rare </div>

While wearing this amulet, one can use an action to cast 'Identify Caster' up to twice a day, resetting at dawn. Its particularly helpful in tracking magic users or in solving mysteries, but no significant usage has been documented, as no significant individual has ever held an amulet like this, oddly enough. 
</div>

<div>
### Totem of Spell Storing
<div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Rare </div>

This totem stores spells cast into it until the spellcaster is ready to use them. It can be a powerful tool to aid those without spellcasting capabilities, provided they have an ally with such abilities. 
</div>

### Sage's Horn
<div className="tiny"> Weapon (Dagger), Uncommon </div>

A broken piece of a wandering Monk's horn, snapped off in a battle with an elite dragon rider. The ridges of the horn glow softly, imbued with her inner Ki. Known to only obey the master it broke off from.

<div> 
## Memory Crystal
<div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Rare </div>

This gem can seemingly remember the places its been. When wielded by a spellcaster it can record a scene before it instantly, as if it could recall the things it had seen. When interacted with, it will show its user the recordings it has taken as reflections in its surface. That is, so long as its surface is kept clear of dust.
</div>

<div> 
## Dimension Blade
<div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Legendary </div>

This crystalline blade can be used as a very powerful ritual focus. If implemented correctly, it can be channeled to be anything from a means of planar travel to an incredible weapon. If implemented incorrectly, it can cause disasters that end up in history books for generations.
</div>
</div>

## Hellfire Owlbear Armor

This armor is for an owlbear of an infernal army. A Tiefling Demon Lord is said to ride one in the massive civil war currently raging through the hells. Though such rumors are ridiculous, and no scholar of academic credit would ever buy into such nonsense.

    ### Matsonian Sealskin Cloak
    <div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Common </div>

    This cloak prevents the wearer from getting soaked, even in the harshest storms. It is commonly found on Kulcinian Sailors, often decorated with the teeth of beasts for clamps or other trophies.     ### Imbued Glass Needle
    <div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Common </div>
    This needle was forged in the heat of the Null Wastes, grown on a fern, and was lucky enough to become imbued with magic. Needles imbued in this way allow even the worst craftsmen to repair small tears or chips in gear.
</div>

<div>
    ### Enchanted Lantern Fruit
    <div className="tiny"> Wondrous Item, Uncommon </div>

    Balbian lantern fruits provide a negligible amount of warmth and light. When enchanted, though, these properties can be isolated and enhanced, and the spoiling of the fruit ended. This lantern provides 30ft of bright light and 30 additional feet of dim light, and creatures within 5 feet are immune to penalties created by nonmagical cold conditions, such as snow or ice. A snowstorm or other significant weather event might be too much, though. The lantern has 4hp and immunity to psychic and poison damage. 
</div>

<div> 
    ### Liar Liar
    <div className="tiny"> Evocation Cantrip </div>

    This trick can be learned by many classes, including all Bards. A creature that failed to lie to the caster is engulfed in flame as retribution. Sucks to suck.

    ### Echo Step
    <div className="tiny"> 3rd Level Conjuration Spell </div>

    The caster creates a spectral copy of themselves, existing in two places at once. A strong enough mage can switch between these two places as they see fit. 
</div>

Known as Arutu's iconic weapon, this Hammer holds a place in the hearts of thousands for good and bad reasons.
