# Item Catalogue

This page is a list of standard mundane items that can be found in Abrigar, not notably magical nor vanilla to the Forgotten Realms. The listed prices indicate how much each item could be realistically sold for. Keep in mind it would be more expensive to purchase an item than the gold gained from selling one.

Items here come with a custom rarity system for trade goods. They have no mechanical bearing but indicate the general difficulty of obtaining that item in Abrigar. The tiers are as follows:

Common, 
Uncommon, 
Rare, 
Exotic, 
Mythical, 
Unique, 

For those items without a rarity, such as dishes and drinks, other information may be listed, such as country of origin, age, or relevant dates.

    <div class="item-subtitle">Rare</div>
  </div>

To mortals, however, it produces intense euphoria, boundless energy, and an addictive craving for more. Prolonged use often leads to paranoia, erratic behavior, and dependency, leaving countless lives ruined in its wake. Despite being outlawed throughout much of Abrigar, Fairy Dust remains one of the world's most profitable illicit trades. Entire criminal syndicates have been built upon its distribution, while public officials are often content to blame the fair folk rather than confront the demand within their own cities.
